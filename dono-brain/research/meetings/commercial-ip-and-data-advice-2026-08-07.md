## Legal, IP and data points for counsel

- A final lawyer review is still awaited. Any review should receive a specific
  brief covering data ownership, user-content/image licensing, marketing use,
  alumni-data handling and the intended platform model.
- Maintain a documented, traceable chain of title for all IP: website, platform,
  data and every contributor transfer.
- For an institution's alumni dataset, obtain written institutional permission
  before use. A lower-risk proposed route is for the institution to send alumni a
  Dono sign-up link so alumni opt in directly.
- College-name use may create endorsement/trademark risk, especially where Dono
  itself creates or promotes a college page. Student-created groups were viewed
  as lower risk. Obtain advice before relying on any position.
- The team intends to consider UK trademark clearance and, when funding permits,
  a filing for **Dono** in Classes 9, 35, 36 and 42. This remains a future task,
  not a clearance result or filing.
- A potentially similar charity-card-machine mark was discussed. Confirm actual
  register results and seek legal advice before approaching any rights holder or
  making a launch decision.

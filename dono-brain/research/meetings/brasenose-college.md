# Meeting with Brasenose College

### Pitching Approach

- When meeting with colleges, address concerns first; do not come across as overly optimistic or "cute"
- Start with the value proposition to colleges as well, student free posting second

### Concerns Raised

- Control of what is posted
- Competition with overall college funds
- Access to data and analytics on donors

### Adoption Dynamics

- Having a college onboard will do bits for other colleges to follow — colleges take inspiration from one another
- The platform is great, but they expect it will be at least 5 years before it is commonplace in the university — the university works slow
- Many colleges will be interested once there is a demo case (e.g. Somerville); Brasenose will likely get on board once this is sorted
- This app is more likely to work better with originally women-only colleges — predominantly male alumni demographics work well on giving days due to the inherently competitive nature of those alumni

### Comparison to Hubbub

- Hubbub is expensive; Dono is free for colleges
- Hubbub uses a create-a-new-site-per-campaign model
- Hubbub is more corporate and creates more work for development offices; Dono is taking on that workload

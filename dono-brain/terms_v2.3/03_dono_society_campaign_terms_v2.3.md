> **Read this first if you are a student officer.** Most student societies are unincorporated associations. Under the law of England and Wales an unincorporated association is not a separate legal person and **cannot itself be a party to a contract**. That means the person who signs up on the Society's behalf is the person Dono contracts with. Clause 1 explains exactly what that does — and, importantly, does not — mean for you personally. **Dono limits your financial exposure to the money in the Connected Account you control**, except where you act dishonestly. Please read clause 1.6 before you accept.

### 1. Who contracts with Dono, and who is liable

1.1 These Terms apply to Campaigns run for a Society, to the Society, to its **Responsible Representative**, and to the **Secondary Contact**.

1.2 **One Responsible Representative.** Every Society Campaign has one Responsible Representative — an individual (for example the President or Treasurer) who creates and manages the Campaign, contracts with Dono, and holds or controls the Connected Account. Dono records their full name, university email and role. Every Society Campaign also registers one **Secondary Contact**, a named current officer who can take over. **The Secondary Contact is not a second approver** and need not complete verification unless and until they take over.

1.3 **Authority warranty.** The Responsible Representative **warrants** that they are authorised under the Society's constitution, governing rules, bank mandate and any applicable Students' Union or institutional requirements to create the Campaign, to authorise the receipt of Donations, and to accept these Terms for and on behalf of the Society.

1.4 **Who the contracting party is.**

(a) **If the Society is incorporated** (for example a company or a charitable incorporated organisation), **the incorporated body is the contracting party** and the Responsible Representative contracts only as its agent. Paragraphs (b) and (c) and clause 1.6 do not apply.

(b) **If the Society is unincorporated**, it has no separate legal personality and cannot be a contracting party. **The Responsible Representative is therefore the contracting party**, contracting for and on behalf of the Society and its members. This is a statement of the legal position under English law, not an additional obligation Dono is imposing.

(c) **The Responsible Representative acts in a representative capacity.** Donations, and anything bought with them, are **raised and held for the benefit of the Society**, not for the representative personally. The representative must not treat Campaign funds or funded property as their own, must keep them separate from their personal money, and must account for them to the Society.

1.4A **How the account is actually opened — please read this carefully.** Where the Society is unincorporated, the Payment Provider cannot open an account in the Society's name, because the Society is not a legal person. **In practice the Responsible Representative — usually the treasurer or the society's principal officer — completes the Payment Provider's onboarding as a sole trader, in their own name.** The consequences are:

(i) the Connected Account is **legally theirs**, and they alone can operate it, refund from it and respond to disputes on it;
(ii) they must nevertheless hold it, and everything in it, **for the Society** (clause 1.4(c));
(iii) the Payment Provider's terms for that account apply to them personally;
(iv) they may have their own reporting obligations to HM Revenue & Customs arising from being registered as a sole trader. **Dono does not advise on this and the Responsible Representative should take their own advice**; and
(v) clause 1.6 limits what Dono may recover from them personally, precisely because the account is in their name.

Dono will explain this at onboarding before the Responsible Representative accepts these Terms.

1.5 **Ordinary obligations remain the Society's in substance.** As between the Society and its Responsible Representative, the obligations under these Terms are intended to be borne by the Society and its funds, and the representative is entitled to be indemnified out of the Society's funds in accordance with its rules. Nothing in these Terms alters the ordinary law governing the internal affairs of an unincorporated association.

1.6 **Limited recourse — what you are actually exposed to.** This clause limits, and does not extend, the personal liability an unincorporated Society's Responsible Representative would otherwise have at law.

(a) **Dono's recourse against a Responsible Representative personally for any ordinary obligation under these Terms — including refunds, surplus, chargebacks, negative balances and unpaid fees — is limited to the funds standing to the credit of the Connected Account they hold or control for the Society, together with any Campaign funds they still hold or have applied otherwise than for the Society's purposes.** Dono will not pursue their personal assets beyond that amount for any such obligation.

(b) **Paragraph (a) does not apply, and the Responsible Representative is personally liable without that limit, where the claim arises from:** their own fraud or dishonesty; their deliberate misuse or misappropriation of Campaign funds or funded property; a material misrepresentation they made, including a breach of the authority warranty in clause 1.3; their deliberate or reckless breach of these Terms; or an obligation that is inherently personal under English law and cannot be limited.

(c) **This clause does not affect the rights of a Donor**, an Institution, a regulator or any other third party, whose claims are governed by the general law.

(d) **This clause is not a guarantee.** Dono does not ask a Responsible Representative to guarantee the obligations of anyone else, and no provision of these Terms operates as one. Nothing automatically transfers the Society's liabilities to the representative beyond the position described in clause 1.4(b).

1.7 **Evidence of authority.** Dono may at any time ask the Responsible Representative for reasonable evidence of their authority — a committee resolution, minutes, the Society's constitution, an authorised-signatory record, or confirmation from the Students' Union or Institution — and may decline to publish, suspend or remove a Campaign until it is provided. **Any internal threshold Dono applies in deciding when to ask for evidence is Dono's own risk control. It does not determine, replace or reduce the approvals the Society's own rules require.**

1.8 **Indemnity for lack of authority.** If the Responsible Representative did not in fact have the authority they warranted in clause 1.3, they will indemnify Dono against any third-party claim, loss, liability or reasonable cost Dono incurs as a direct result. Clause 28.3 of the Terms of Service (conduct of a claim, mitigation and proportionate reduction) applies. **This indemnity is limited to the consequences of the lack of authority and does not extend to anything else.**

1.9 **A Society is not a business user.** For the purposes of clauses 27 and 28 of the Terms of Service, a student society, club or similar non-commercial group is a **Society** — a distinct category — and is not treated as a business user merely because it is an organisation. Clause 27.3(b) applies to the Society. **An individual acting as a Responsible Representative is a consumer in their own right**, and clause 27.3(a) applies to any claim they bring for their own loss. Business-specific provisions apply only where the user is genuinely acting in the course of a trade, business, craft or profession, or is an incorporated commercial organisation.

1.10 **The Society is the beneficiary.** Funds are raised for the Society and the funded property or output belongs to the Society under the Ownership Statement. Society approval and the appearance of a Society or institution name on a Campaign do not mean the university, college or institution operates, sponsors or endorses the Campaign or Dono.

### 2. Creating and approving a Society Campaign

2.1 A Society Campaign must be approved through the relevant society page before it is presented as a society campaign. Nothing becomes public automatically; every Campaign is reviewed by a person before publication.

2.2 **Authority declaration.** On approval the Responsible Representative actively confirms:

> *"I confirm that I am authorised to create this Campaign, to authorise the receipt of Donations, and to accept these Terms for and on behalf of the Society; and that every approval required by the Society's constitution, governing rules, bank mandate and any applicable Students' Union or institutional requirement has been obtained."*

2.3 **One approver is sufficient for Dono to publish.** Dono does not require a second person to approve. **The approvals the Society itself needs are those its own rules require** — Dono does not set them, does not reduce them, and does not certify that they were obtained. Dono may request documentary evidence under clause 1.7 at any time, and will ordinarily do so for higher-value or higher-risk Campaigns. Satisfying Dono's checks does not mean Dono endorses the Campaign or that the Society has complied with its own constitution.

2.4 **No public status indicators.** Dono displays no "Society approved" badge, verification tick or comparable indicator. Approval is an internal step.

2.5 Society Campaigns must comply with clause 8 of the Terms of Service. **Fundraising for a charity, a charitable cause, a third party or the general public is prohibited** — a Society Campaign must fund the Society's own activity (Terms of Service clause 8.4). Religious and cultural society activities are permitted; alcohol-related expenditure is prohibited, including as part of a society event. **Recurring donations, matched-funding windows and commercial fundraising are not available.**

### 3. Recipient, ownership and split roles

3.1 **The expected structure.** The Society is the Campaign Owner and the beneficiary; the Connected Account is held by the Society if it is incorporated, and otherwise **by the Responsible Representative for the Society**; and anything purchased belongs to the Society. **Dono expects these to align, and designs for that case.**

3.2 **Ownership Statement.** At setup the Campaign records who will legally own the funded property or output. The Responsible Representative warrants that it is accurate and that they have authority to make it. Funded items must be used for the stated purpose. **An outgoing officer must not retain funded property personally**, and the proceeds of any sale remain dedicated to Society purposes.

3.3 **Split roles are exceptional.** Where there is a genuine operational need for the Campaign Owner, the Recipient and the owner of funded property to differ, Dono may permit it only under an **express written agreement signed by every affected party before publication**, recording: the intended beneficiary of the Donations; ownership of any purchased assets; permitted use of the funds; responsibility for refunds; record-keeping obligations; succession where office-holders change; and responsibility for disputes. All roles are then shown at checkout under clause 11.5 of the Terms of Service. **A registered charity may not hold the Connected Account or funded property for a Society**, because that structure is inconsistent with clause 8.4 of the Terms of Service.

3.4 Whether a given Society or representative can in practice open the required Stripe connected account is confirmed before its first Campaign is published.

### 4. Responsibilities and refunds

4.1 The Responsible Representative must: maintain and privately submit evidence within 14 days of expenditure, redacted as required by clause 7.2 of the Student Campaign Terms; publish required updates and a Closure Statement; **notify Material Changes before implementing them and not spend the affected funds until the refund window closes**; respond within **ten Working Days**, extendable on reasonable request; and cooperate with refunds and investigations.

4.2 **Refund execution and Dono's mandate.** The Connected Account holder must execute promptly any refund Dono determines is due. **In addition, by accepting these Terms the Society and the Responsible Representative irrevocably authorise Dono, as their agent for that limited purpose only, to instruct the Payment Provider to reverse the charge, in whole or in part, from the Connected Account** (Terms of Service clause 13.2). Dono will normally do so only after notifying the Responsible Representative and giving them the opportunity to respond, except where a Donation must be reversed urgently. **A determination may be appealed within ten Working Days of notification**, but the refund may already have been executed. Dono never holds Society funds and never takes money for itself under this mandate; where a refund is due and the Donor was not at fault, Dono separately reverses its own fee.

4.3 The Connected Account is charged the Payment Provider's processing and dispute fees, **owns any dispute, and is responsible for submitting evidence** to the Payment Provider. Dono notifies the account holder as soon as it becomes aware of a dispute, alerts them before the card-network deadline, and provides the records it holds. **No double recovery:** if a Donor both receives a refund and succeeds in a chargeback for the same Donation, the duplicate amount is recoverable and must be reported to Dono promptly.

4.4 Receipts and evidence are held privately and never shown to Donors; the campaign page shows a neutral lifecycle state only.

4.4A **Comments on a Society Campaign may be posted only by approved members of that Society** (clause 17.1 of the Terms of Service). The Society controls its own membership, and therefore controls who may comment. A Society Leader may hide a comment on the Society's own Campaign; Dono retains a moderation copy and may restore it where removal is misused.

4.5 **Donor identity** is not disclosed by Dono to the Recipient or the Society as a dispute-resolution step; disclosure occurs only where required or permitted by law. **Separately**, because Donations are charged directly to the Connected Account, the Payment Provider may show the account holder transaction details including a Donor's name, whatever their display preference on Dono. That is outside Dono's control, and using it to identify, contact or pressure a Donor who chose to hide their name is a breach of these Terms. Access to the Connected Account must be limited to officers who need it.

4.6 **Fees and tax records.** **The Society is Dono's customer for the platform service in every case.** Dono's fee is **5% + 20p** and is separate from the Payment Provider's processing cost, which is charged to the Connected Account. Donations are paid to the Recipient and are not Dono's revenue. **Dono is not VAT registered and issues no VAT invoices**; it provides a statement of its fee and any reversal. A freely given contribution for which the Donor receives nothing in return is generally outside the scope of VAT, but a charitable-sounding purpose does not make a Society a charity, and the treatment of the Society's receipts depends on its own legal status and activities. The Society and the Recipient must retain the donation, fee, refund, dispute and expenditure records their own accounts and tax position require.

### 5. Resale and disposal

5.1 Clause 8 of the Student Campaign Terms applies: no acquisition intended for immediate resale or personal profit; no prior approval required to dispose, but any disposal of a material asset must be notified to Dono within 14 days with the reason, proceeds and application; pre-completion sale proceeds must be reapplied or refunded; disposal after genuine use is permitted. **Proceeds remain dedicated to Society purposes and may not be retained by an individual officer.**

### 6. Succession — changing the Responsible Representative

6.1 **The rule is simple: a Society must always have a current Responsible Representative.** When the person in that role changes, the Society must appoint a replacement who completes Dono's onboarding and becomes the new Responsible Representative.

6.2 **What must happen.** The incoming representative verifies control of a university email address at a Recognised Institution, **completes the Payment Provider's identity check**, gives the authority declaration in clause 2.2, and accepts these Terms. The Society confirms that the change was made in accordance with its own rules; Dono may request evidence under clause 1.7.

6.2A **How a change is made.** A Society starts a change of Responsible Representative through the Platform's handover process. Dono records the incoming representative's completed onboarding and the Society's confirmation before approving the change. The existing representative remains in place until approval is complete.

6.3 **Cooperation by the outgoing representative.** The outgoing representative must reasonably cooperate in transferring the Connected Account (where they hold it), campaign records, evidence, outstanding correspondence and any unresolved obligations, and must account to the Society for any Campaign funds they hold.

6.4 **Dono's controls during a handover.** Until the transition is complete, **Dono may prevent the Society from creating new Campaigns and may suspend a Campaign to new Donations.** Dono cannot suspend or delay a payout from a Connected Account and does not claim to be able to.

6.5 **Responsibility during and after a handover.**

(a) **Live Campaigns** continue, and the Society remains responsible for them throughout. The incoming representative assumes responsibility for the Campaign from the date the change is approved.
(b) **The outgoing representative remains responsible for everything that happened before the change**, including transactions processed through a Connected Account they held, refunds and chargebacks arising from those transactions, evidence for expenditure they made, and their own conduct. Clause 1.6 continues to limit their exposure on the same basis.
(c) **Refund obligations do not move with the office.** A refund arising from a Donation received before the change remains executable from the Connected Account that received it, and Dono's mandate under clause 4.2 continues to apply to that account.
(d) **Compliance obligations** — evidence, updates, the Closure Statement and cooperation with investigations — pass to the incoming representative, who must obtain what they need from their predecessor. Where the predecessor fails to cooperate, that is a breach of clause 6.3 and a ground for enforcement.

6.6 **Where a Connected Account cannot be transferred.** The Payment Provider does not support moving a balance from one connected account to another. Where the incoming representative must open a new Connected Account, **funds already received remain in the outgoing representative's account and must be dealt with by them under these Terms** — spent on the campaign purpose, or refunded. Any transfer of money between the two individuals and the Society is a matter between them, outside Dono and the Payment Provider, and Dono does not arrange, supervise or guarantee it. Dono will say this plainly at setup, and the Campaign is paused to new Donations while a change of Connected Account is completed.

6.7 A change of Responsible Representative, of the Society, or of the Connected Account is a Material Change and follows clause 14.4 of the Terms of Service.

### 7. Failed recovery and enforcement

7.1 If a refund a Donor is entitled to is not executed, the Connected Account holder remains contractually liable, subject to clause 1.6. **Dono never pays refunds from its own funds and does not guarantee recovery.** Dono may suspend or ban users, remove Campaigns, prevent future fundraising on Dono, refer deliberate misuse to the Institution under the Institutional Referral Protocol, and report suspected fraud to the police or another authority. **Dono cannot withhold or delay a payout from a Connected Account.**

7.2 Dono may warn, restrict, suspend, remove or ban the Campaign, the Society's presence, the Responsible Representative, the Secondary Contact or the Connected Account holder under Part 9 of the Terms of Service.

### 8. General

8.1 The changes, termination, governing-law, complaints and general provisions of the Terms of Service apply. **Liability follows clause 27.3(b) for the Society and clause 27.3(a) for an individual representative's own consumer claim.** The only indemnities that apply are the narrow indemnity in clause 28.2 of the Terms of Service and the lack-of-authority indemnity in clause 1.8 above. Governed by the law of England and Wales.

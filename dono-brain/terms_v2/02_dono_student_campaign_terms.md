### 1. About these Terms

1.1 These Student Campaign Terms apply to individual students who create or operate a Campaign on Dono ("you"). They supplement the Dono Terms of Service, which continue to apply, and take precedence over the Terms of Service where they conflict. Defined terms have the meanings given in the Terms of Service.

1.2 Dono is a trading name operated by Amrit Kaur Rooprai, a sole trader, of 37 St Giles', Oxford OX1 3LD. Dono does not hold campaign funds, does not itself process card payments, and is not affiliated with any university, college or society unless expressly stated in writing.

1.3 If your Campaign is a Society Campaign, the Society Campaign Terms also apply and take precedence over these Terms where they conflict.

### 2. Eligibility

2.1 To create and operate a Campaign you must, at the time of creation and throughout the life of the Campaign:

(a) be at least 18;
(b) be currently enrolled at a Recognised Institution;
(c) be physically studying in the UK;
(d) hold and use a valid university email address;
(e) provide an acceptable student-card image;
(f) complete the Payment Provider's Connected Account onboarding; and
(g) remain eligible while the Campaign is active.

2.2 Visiting students and students on placement are eligible, provided they meet the other requirements.

2.3 **Interruption and leave of absence.** A student on interruption or an approved leave of absence remains enrolled and is eligible, provided they declare that they expect to return to study **before the Campaign End Date**. Dono relies on your declaration. A declaration you know to be untrue is a breach of these Terms and of the Terms of Service, and is a ground for refusal, suspension, removal and refund.

2.4 **Suspension.** A student who is suspended by their institution is **not eligible** to create or operate a Campaign while the suspension lasts. You must tell Dono promptly if you are suspended while a Campaign is active. Clause 12.4 explains what happens to a live Campaign in that case.

2.5 The following are **not** eligible: recent graduates; people who have left their institution; people whose current student status cannot be verified; and students attempting to create a Campaign within 60 days of their Course End Date.

2.6 **Course End Date.** Where your course ends before your graduation ceremony, your Course End Date governs for the purposes of these Terms, including the 60-day rules in clauses 2.5 and 12.1 and the closing requirement in clause 5.2. This is because the Course End Date is the point at which you cease to be reachable through, and accountable to, your institution.

2.7 The Platform requires you to be physically studying in the UK and to hold a Connected Account. Part-time and distance-learning students studying from outside the UK do not meet clause 2.1(c).

### 3. Student-status checks, reverification and age

3.1 To create a Campaign you must submit a valid university email address and an acceptable student-card image. Dono administrators manually review the card and check your current student status. Dono aims to complete its review within seven business days.

3.2 The Payment Provider separately conducts its own identity and KYC onboarding for your Connected Account. Dono does not conduct those checks. The Payment Provider's onboarding status is recorded internally by Dono and is not displayed publicly.

3.3 **Reverification.** Your student status is reverified **each October**, or on the expiry date shown on your student card if that date falls earlier. If you completed a Student Status Check within the three months before a scheduled reverification, you are not reverified until the following cycle. While a reverification is pending and in time, your Campaign continues to run normally.

3.4 Dono may request further information at any time. Unless a different period is stated, you must respond within **five working days**. Failure to respond may result in refusal, suspension or removal.

3.5 You declare your date of birth to Dono. Your student card is not relied on as proof of age. The Payment Provider's KYC process is intended to confirm identity and age eligibility. **[DEVELOPER INPUT REQUIRED — confirm the reliability of the age-check signal in the final implementation]**

3.6 The label "**Student status checked by Dono**" means only that Dono has completed the limited checks in this clause 3. It does not verify every campaign statement, the authenticity of every document, the accuracy of every cost, the viability of your Project, your future conduct, institutional endorsement, or successful completion.

### 4. Creating your Campaign

4.1 Your Campaign must include: a campaign name; a clear explanation of what is being funded and why the money is needed; a detailed itemised budget with accurate estimated costs; purchase links where applicable; a funding target; a Campaign End Date; an expected purchase or expenditure date; supporting media and documents where appropriate; a statement of who will own the funded property or output; and a planned update schedule.

4.2 You must accurately represent the Campaign in both the campaign application and the public campaign page, and must comply with the campaign rules and permitted categories in clause 8 of the Terms of Service and the Community Guidelines.

4.3 You must not fundraise on behalf of another individual and must not operate a pass-through campaign for another person or organisation. Fundraising on behalf of a Society is governed by the Society Campaign Terms.

4.4 If your Campaign relates to a commercial venture, clause 8.4 of the Terms of Service applies. Such Campaigns are permitted but discouraged, are scrutinised closely, and must state prominently that Donors receive no equity, ownership, repayment right or financial return while any value created accrues to you.

### 5. Campaign duration and the Campaign End Date

5.1 Every Campaign must have a funding-closing date — the Campaign End Date — after which no further Donations may be made.

5.2 A Campaign may ordinarily remain open for no more than 12 months, and must ordinarily close at least 60 days before your Course End Date. Dono may approve an extension at its discretion.

5.3 Donations are charged immediately and are collected even if the funding target is not met.

### 6. Partial funding

6.1 If your Campaign does not reach its target, you decide whether the Project can proceed with the amount raised.

6.2 If the money raised can still be meaningfully used for the stated campaign purpose, the Project may proceed. If it cannot be meaningfully used, you must arrange refunds in accordance with the Refund and Dispute Policy. Insufficient funding alone does not automatically cancel the Project.

### 7. Permitted use of funds and campaign changes

7.1 **Core rule.** Campaign funds may be used only for the purposes represented to Donors on the campaign page, including approved line items and reasonable directly related expenditure.

7.2 **Reallocation.** You may move funds between verified line items, provided the reallocation remains within the same stated campaign purpose. A material diversion to a different purpose is prohibited without Dono approval and any necessary donor notice or refund.

7.3 **Adding or changing line items.** You may: add a line item after publication, subject to Dono review; update a price where a source or reasonable evidence is provided; and buy a substitute item serving substantially the same purpose and costing a similar or lower amount.

7.4 **Material Changes.** A Material Change (as defined in the Terms of Service) requires, before implementation: an explanation; evidence or a source; Dono review; a campaign-page update; donor notice where appropriate; and may give rise to donor refund rights.

### 8. Surplus Funds

8.1 Funds may be moved between verified line items and used for reasonable additional expenditure directly advancing the stated campaign purpose. Any amount that cannot reasonably be used for that purpose must be refunded.

8.2 Surplus is refunded in reverse chronological order of donation: the most recent Donor first, up to the amount they donated, then the next most recent, until the surplus is exhausted. ### 9. Evidence, updates and closure

9.1 You must declare when you plan to make purchases.

9.2 You must submit receipts or other evidence of expenditure to Dono within **14 days** of each expenditure.

9.3 **Evidence is private.** Receipts and evidence you submit are held privately by Dono. They are **not published on your campaign page and are not shown to Donors.** What Donors see is the factual evidence status described in clause 9.7.

9.4 **Redaction before submission.** Before submitting a receipt or other evidence you must remove:

(a) full payment card numbers, card security codes and full bank account or sort code details;
(b) the names, addresses and contact details of any third party who is not a supplier acting in the course of business;
(c) any information relating to a purchase that is not part of the Campaign, where it appears on the same document; and
(d) any special category information (for example information revealing health, religious belief or sexual orientation) belonging to you or anyone else, unless it is inherent in the campaign purpose itself.

You do **not** need to redact supplier names, item descriptions, prices, dates, order references or VAT details. Dono needs those to review the expenditure against your budget. If a document cannot be redacted without destroying its usefulness, contact Dono before submitting it.

9.5 **Updates.** You must publish a progress update at least once for every three months of active fundraising, and an outcome update approximately **three months** after expenditure. You set your own milestone dates when the Campaign is approved; they may be changed only with Dono's approval.

9.6 **Closure Statement.** You must conclude your Campaign by publishing a Closure Statement setting out what was done, what was spent, and the final position on any unspent funds. This is mandatory. Once Dono accepts your Closure Statement, your update obligations end. Your evidence, refund, cooperation and investigation obligations continue under clause 31.3 of the Terms of Service.

9.7 **Evidence status shown to Donors.** Your campaign page displays one of the following, and nothing more: *evidence of expenditure received and reviewed*; *evidence outstanding*; or *evidence overdue*. The first means only that Dono has received evidence for the expenditure you declared and has reviewed it against your stated budget. It is not a statement by Dono that you spent the money properly.

9.8 Dono sends reminders before taking enforcement action. One missed deadline triggers follow-up by email and on-platform notice. Continued failure may result in suspension or removal of your Campaign or account.

9.9 Evidence, receipts and dispute records are retained for **six years**, as set out in the Privacy Policy.

### 10. Funded property and resale

10.1 Unless the campaign page states otherwise, the funded item or output belongs to you and must be used for the stated campaign purpose.

10.2 An item must not be acquired with the intention of immediate resale or personal profit.

10.3 An item may not normally be sold within 12 months of acquisition without Dono's prior approval. Earlier sale may be permitted where the item is defective, unsuitable, replaced or no longer reasonably needed, for a documented reason.

10.4 If an item is sold before the campaign purpose is completed, the proceeds must be reapplied to that purpose or refunded. Disposal after genuine use for the campaign purpose is permitted.

### 11. Refunds, chargebacks and cooperation

11.1 Donors may seek refunds on the grounds and within the time limits set out in the Donor Terms and the Refund and Dispute Policy. You must respond to a refund request or related information request within **five working days**. Dono aims to decide a refund request within 21 calendar days of receiving a complete request.

11.2 **You execute approved refunds.** Where Dono approves a refund, **you must execute it promptly from your own Stripe dashboard.** Dono decides that a refund is owed; you carry it out. Failure to execute an approved refund promptly is a breach of these Terms.

The Donor may pursue chargeback, Payment Provider or legal remedies against you.

### 12. Graduation, departure and loss of status

12.1 You must not start a new Campaign within 60 days of your Course End Date.

12.2 Active Campaigns must close, complete or (in the case of Society Campaigns only) be properly transferred before you leave your institution. Individual Campaigns cannot be transferred to another person, because the Connected Account and the money in it belong to you and cannot be moved between accounts.

12.3 Graduation or departure does not extinguish your refund, evidence, update, Closure Statement, cooperation or investigation obligations.

12.4 **Loss of student status during a Campaign.** If you lose student status before your Campaign concludes — through graduation, withdrawal, suspension, transfer or otherwise — your Campaign closes to new Donations, and any funds not already properly spent on the stated campaign purpose must be refunded in accordance with the Refund and Dispute Policy. You remain the Merchant of Record for Donations already received and remain bound by all continuing obligations.

### 13. Enforcement

13.1 Dono may warn, restrict, suspend, remove or ban in accordance with Part 10 of the Terms of Service, including for false information, fraud or suspected fraud, failure to provide evidence, updates or a Closure Statement, misuse of funds, unpaid approved refunds, Community Guidelines breaches, Payment Provider suspension or loss of student status. Dono cannot necessarily pause a payout already sent or recover money already withdrawn.

### 14. Your responsibility

14.1 You are responsible for: the truth and accuracy of your Campaign; your authority to run it; lawful conduct; costs and line items; proper use of funds; evidence and redaction; updates and your Closure Statement; the funded property and its disposal; refunds and their execution; chargebacks; cooperation with Dono and the Payment Provider; and claims caused by your fraud, illegality, misrepresentation or intellectual-property infringement.

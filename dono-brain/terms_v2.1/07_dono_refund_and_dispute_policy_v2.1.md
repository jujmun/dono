## 1. Read this first

1.1 Dono does not hold your money. Your Donation goes directly to the Campaign Owner's (the Recipient's) own Stripe connected account. Dono takes a platform fee and never receives, holds or controls the donation itself.

1.2 **Dono determines whether, under the Platform's rules, a Campaign Owner is required to issue a refund.** Dono is deciding whether the Campaign Owner has complied with their obligations — it is not awarding damages. A refund the Donor is entitled to is executed by the Campaign Owner from their own Stripe account. Dono has sanctions and escalation routes, but **cannot compel a refund to happen** once funds have left the payment system, and never pays refunds from its own funds.

1.3 Each Donation creates a direct contract between the Donor and the Campaign Owner about the use of donated funds and any refund obligations. Dono is not a party to that contract; Dono administers the Platform and this process under its own Terms.

1.4 Nothing here removes your statutory rights, your rights against your card issuer, or your rights under the Payment Provider's terms.

## 2. Two different routes

2.1 A **refund request** goes to Dono, which investigates and determines whether the Campaign Owner must refund; the Campaign Owner executes it. A **chargeback** goes to your bank or card issuer, which decides under card-scheme rules; Dono only supplies evidence and cannot stop it.

2.2 **Please contact Dono first.** In most cases we can investigate and, where appropriate, arrange a refund more quickly than a card dispute. Using Dono's process does not prevent a later chargeback, and **using a lawful chargeback does not breach these Terms.** A chargeback should generally be a last resort if the issue cannot be resolved directly with us — this is guidance, not a restriction. Note that our 21-day target may consume part of your issuer's chargeback window; if timing matters, check your issuer's deadlines.

2.3 Give accurate information in any refund request or chargeback. Knowingly making a false or fraudulent claim may result in suspension and other appropriate action.

## 3. Grounds for a refund

3.1 Dono may determine a refund is required on grounds including: duplicate payment; payment error; unauthorised payment; cancellation of the Campaign; material misrepresentation; fraud; misuse of funds; failure to provide required evidence; a Material Change made without authorisation; inability to use the money meaningfully for the stated purpose; failure to proceed where funds should be returned; failure or withdrawal of a student-status check; or failure to publish a Closure Statement after reminders.

3.2 **Not** grounds on their own: disappointment with the outcome; a Project attempted honestly that did not succeed; minor deviations; a change of mind; or the target not being met where the money can still be used meaningfully. Campaigns describe the **intended** use of funds; they are not promises of a result.

## 4. Decision framework

4.1 Decisions are made on the **balance of probabilities** (more likely than not), considering all relevant evidence and weighing conflicting evidence.

4.2 A refund is justified only where the issue **materially affected** the donation or campaign outcome, and there must be a **causal connection** between the alleged breach and the refund requested.

4.3 Dono may decide on no refund, a partial refund or a full refund; partial refunds reflect the extent of the issue, and cases of shared responsibility are apportioned. Similar cases are decided consistently, with internal review where appropriate.

4.4 **Conflicts of interest.** Any member of the Dono team involved in a dispute must declare it and recuse themselves from deciding it.

## 5. Time, completeness and the process

5.1 A request is submitted through the Platform with a reason and any supporting material. A request is **complete** once Dono has the information reasonably required to assess it; if more is needed, Dono will normally identify everything it reasonably requires in a **single** request, as soon as reasonably practicable, and reserves the right to seek more only if genuinely necessary.

5.2 **Time limits** (for Dono's voluntary internal process only; they do not shorten your legal rights): an ordinary request within **60 days** after the Campaign End Date, or after you became aware of the matter, whichever is later; a fraud or deliberate-misrepresentation request up to **12 months** after the Donation. Dono may accept late cases where fair; latent-fraud and legal rights are preserved.

5.3 **Timescales.** Dono aims to acknowledge a request within **two Working Days**. Dono decides within **21 calendar days** of receiving a complete request. Where a decision cannot reasonably be made in that time because of complexity or delay in obtaining information, Dono may extend the period **once by up to a further 21 calendar days**, notifying the parties of the reason and an updated expected date. **External deadlines — card-scheme chargeback windows, statutory limitation periods and any other external deadlines — continue independently of Dono's process.**

5.4 **The Campaign Owner's response.** The Campaign Owner is told the substance of the allegation and shown the evidence relied on, and has **five Working Days** to respond, with a reasonable extension available on request and urgent interim measures where needed. No adverse inference is drawn unless the Campaign Owner received the notice and had a fair opportunity to respond.

5.5 **Identity.** Where the Donor hid their name, their identity is withheld from the Campaign Owner. Dono does not disclose it as a dispute-resolution step. Disclosure occurs only where required or permitted by law, following clause 25 of the Terms of Service (minimum necessary, documented necessity and proportionality, legal review, notice to the affected user where lawful).

5.6 Both parties receive the outcome and reasons. An appeal may be brought within **ten Working Days** and is reviewed by a different member of the Dono team where reasonably practicable, applying conflict-of-interest and recusal rules. The appeal is Dono's final internal decision and does not affect your statutory, card-issuer or legal rights.

## 6. How an approved refund is paid

6.1 **The Campaign Owner executes the refund from their own Stripe dashboard.** Dono does not process or initiate the refund through the Payment Provider's API, and holds no reserve power to do so.

6.2 A Campaign Owner notified that a refund is required is contractually obliged to execute it promptly. Failure is a breach of the Campaign Terms and a ground for enforcement under Part 10 of the Terms of Service. A refund may be delayed or fail where the Connected Account holds insufficient funds; Dono does not make up the difference.

6.3 **Refunds and exchange rates.** A refund returns the sterling amount donated; a non-UK card issuer converts it back at the refund-day rate, so the sum received in the Donor's currency may differ slightly. Neither Dono nor the Campaign Owner controls that rate.

## 7. If the Campaign Owner does not cooperate

7.1 Where a refund the Donor is entitled to is not executed, Dono may, in escalating order: suspend or remove the Campaign and suspend or permanently ban the account; prevent future fundraising on Dono; **withhold future payouts that have not yet been released, where the payment flow allows** **[CONFIRM WITH STRIPE]**; escalate to the Recognised Institution through Dono's institutional contact channel and record that correspondence; support the Donor's chargeback by assembling evidence; and report suspected fraud to the police or another authority.

7.2 Dono does not pursue debt collection and does not guarantee recovery. The Campaign Owner remains contractually liable to the Donor, who may pursue chargeback, Payment Provider or legal remedies, including through appropriate legal channels where they need the Campaign Owner's identity.

## 8. Chargebacks

8.1 A chargeback is decided by the Donor's card issuer under network rules; neither Dono nor the Payment Provider makes the substantive decision, and Dono cannot prevent or reverse one. The Connected Account bears the chargeback and its consequences. **[CONFIRM WITH STRIPE — negative-balance and losses-coverage settings; who submits evidence and who pays dispute fees]**

8.2 Where a chargeback follows a request Dono rejected, Dono assembles and submits the defence evidence on the Campaign Owner's behalf. Dono assists; it does not decide.

## 9. Fees on a refund

9.1 Dono's platform fee is the Payment Provider's applicable cost plus 3.5 percentage points, of which the 3.5 percentage points is Dono's own fee (see clause 16 of the Terms of Service).

9.2 **Dono refunds its own platform fee where a refund is due and the Donor was not at fault.** Applying that: where a Campaign cannot proceed, or the Campaign Owner caused the refund through mistake, breach or misrepresentation, the Donor receives Dono's fee back and the Campaign Owner bears the economic cost so far as technically possible; where the Donor caused the refund through their own error, Dono's fee is not refunded; on a partial refund, Dono's fee may be refunded proportionately where the Donor was not at fault. A fee-cover amount the Donor paid is treated the same way as Dono's fee.

9.3 What the Payment Provider can technically reverse (including its processing cost and any application-fee reversal), and the exact two-step mechanics of the Campaign Owner's charge refund and Dono's separate fee refund, are confirmed against the live configuration before publication. The published clause states clearly and separately: what the Donor actually receives; who bears the economic cost; and what the Payment Provider can technically reverse. **[CONFIRM WITH STRIPE]**

## 10. Failed, cancelled and partially funded Campaigns; surplus

10.1 Not reaching target does not automatically cancel a Project; the Campaign Owner decides whether it can still proceed meaningfully, otherwise refunds are arranged. A Campaign cancelled before the money is spent is refunded.

10.2 **Surplus.** Any amount that cannot reasonably be used for the stated purpose is refunded in **reverse chronological order** — the most recent Donor first, up to the amount they donated, then the next, until the surplus is exhausted. Surplus is **not** shared proportionately, and no Donor is guaranteed a share; this is disclosed at checkout.

10.3 **De minimis.** A threshold applies below which a residual amount need not be refunded (for example, where a £1,000 campaign raises £1,001, the remaining £1 need not be refunded). The threshold accounts for the 20p fixed component and the Payment Provider's per-transaction charges, which can make very small refunds uneconomic. **[TO BE CALCULATED BY DONO TEAM — set the figure(s); disclose at checkout and closure.]**

## 11. Evidence, updates and closure

11.1 Campaign Owners declare expected purchase dates, submit receipts privately within 14 days of expenditure (redacted per the Campaign Terms; Dono may itself redact or remove unnecessary personal data), provide updates, and submit a Closure Statement under clause 10.3 of the Terms of Service (30-day submission, defined contents, 30-day review, limited rejection grounds, one cure cycle, deemed acceptance, subject to fraud investigations).

11.2 What Donors see is the factual evidence status only: "Documents received; line-item amounts matched to budget; documents not authenticated", *evidence outstanding*, or *evidence overdue*. Failure to provide evidence (clause 3.1) and failure to publish a Closure Statement are grounds for a refund and for enforcement.

Complaints about other matters (account suspension, campaign removal, verification, technical issues, moderation, fees) are handled under Dono's separate **Complaints Policy**, referenced in clause 33 of the Terms of Service. **[INTERNAL/PUBLIC DOCUMENT — see tasks list]**

12.2 Dono retains refund and dispute records (requests, evidence, decisions, reasons, appeals, escalation) and receipts and evidence of expenditure for **six years**, reflecting the limitation period for contract claims in England and Wales. See the Privacy Notice.

## 1. What cookies are

1.1 Cookies are small files placed on your device; similar technologies (local storage, session storage, pixels and SDKs) do comparable things. In this Notice, "cookies" covers all of them. This Notice sits alongside the Privacy Notice.

## 2. The law we work to

2.1 Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR), we may set cookies that are **strictly necessary** for a service you asked for without consent. For other technologies a **purpose-and-conditions test** applies: **tracking or profiling analytics require your consent**; certain **privacy-preserving statistical analytics** may fall within the statistical-purpose exception if the statutory conditions are met and a simple objection route is provided. We do not treat third-party tracking as exempt. Consent must be freely given, specific and informed, and as easy to refuse and withdraw as to give, and **cannot be obtained through the Terms and Conditions.**

## 3. Categories we use

3.1 **Strictly necessary — no consent required.** Authentication and session cookies; security and fraud-prevention cookies; cookies set by the Payment Provider during checkout; and a cookie recording your cookie preferences.

3.2 **Statistical analytics — objection available.** Dono uses limited analytics to produce aggregate statistics about how visitors use the Platform and to improve its content, functionality and performance. These are **not** used to identify or profile individual visitors, personalise content, support advertising, or track visitors across services. Where this processing involves personal data, Dono relies on its legitimate interests, subject to a documented balancing assessment; individual-level information is retained only as long as necessary to produce aggregate statistics and then deleted or irreversibly anonymised. What we collect at most: number of visits and page views; which pages and campaign pages are viewed; aggregate clicks and scroll depth; time spent, bounce and exit pages; referring website or campaign; device, browser and OS types; page-loading and error performance; coarse region or city-level location; and aggregate A/B-test results.

3.3 **Advertising and tracking.** Dono does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If this changes, this Notice is updated and consent obtained first.

## 5. Your choices

5.1 **Statistical analytics notice (default).** Where analytics operate under the statistical-purpose exception, we show a clear notice — "We collect limited information about how visitors use Dono, such as pages viewed, aggregate interactions, device type and page performance. We use it only to produce statistics and improve the Platform. We do not use it to identify you, build a profile, advertise to you or track you across other websites." — with a simple, free **[Turn off analytics]** control that actually stops the relevant collection, and a permanent "Privacy and analytics settings" link to change your choice later.

5.2 **Consent banner (when required).** For anything that needs consent — recording an identifiable visitor's full journey, session replay, persistent individual profiles, linking browsing to a later account or donation, interest-based personalisation, cross-site or cross-device tracking, advertising conversions, precise location, retaining individual-level analytics after aggregation, or allowing a provider to use the data for its own purposes — we show an equal-choice banner (**[Accept analytics] [Reject analytics] [Manage choices]**) before the technology activates. "Accept" and "Reject" are equally prominent, nothing optional loads before acceptance, and consent can be withdrawn as easily as it was given.

5.3 **Your browser.** You can block or delete cookies through your browser; blocking strictly necessary cookies stops parts of the Platform working. We record your consent choice and when you made it (see the Privacy Notice for retention).

## 6. Server logs

6.1 Dono retains proportionate server logs (IP address, timestamp, requested page, browser and error data) for security, fraud prevention, service availability, fault detection and incident investigation. These normally do not require cookie consent where they do not involve non-exempt storage or access on your device; we rely on legitimate interests, limit retention and restrict access, and do not repurpose security logs for behavioural analytics without reassessing the position.

# Dono Verification Notice

**Version:** Working draft v2.1 — 30 July 2026
**Status:** DRAFT — NOT FOR PUBLICATION. Applies the counsel review and founder decisions; requires final UK solicitor sign-off. Open items marked **[SOLICITOR SIGN-OFF]**, **[CONFIRM WITH ENGINEERING]**.

> **This is a Notice, not a contract.** It explains Dono's checks and the statuses shown on the Platform. It does not create contractual rights or obligations. Operative obligations that previously sat here (notifying changes in student status; succession) now live in the Student and Society Campaign Terms.

> **What changed from v0.2:** reclassified as a Notice (no longer "incorporated into the Terms of Service"); framed around **one mandatory pre-publication validation** rather than trust badges; "Verified" badges removed; the "reviewed" evidence status reworded so it cannot be read as authentication; the student-card **image is not retained** (the card number is kept); operative status-change and succession duties moved into the Campaign Terms.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 The word "verified" means different things to different people, so this Notice explains exactly what Dono checks, what it does not, what others check, and what each status shown on the Platform means. Nothing on the Platform should lead a Donor to believe Dono has confirmed more than it actually has.

## 2. One mandatory validation before publication

2.1 **Every Campaign completes the same standard validation before it can be published.** There are no "Verified Student" or "Verified Campaign" badges. The validation checks whether a Campaign meets Dono's publication requirements — it is not an endorsement, guarantee, audit or certification.

2.2 The validation consists of:

(a) **Identity and student-status checking** (clause 3);
(b) **Required documents** for the campaign type;
(c) **Campaign completeness** — that the required fields (clause 7.1 of the Terms of Service) are present and internally consistent; and
(d) for Society Campaigns, **society approval** and the authority declaration (clause 2 of the Society Campaign Terms).

2.3 Publication means only that these requirements were met. It does **not** confirm the accuracy of campaign statements, the future use of funds, or the authenticity of every supporting document unless expressly stated.

## 3. The student-status check

3.1 To create a Campaign an applicant provides a valid university email address at a Recognised Institution, an image of a current student card (for one-off checking only), their name, declared date of birth, institution and college, course, student number, card expiry date, and Course End Date.

3.2 A Dono administrator manually reviews the card image against the information provided and the university email, aiming to complete review within seven Working Days (a target, not a commitment). Dono may request further information (respond within five Working Days) and may refuse a check where it reasonably suspects fraud, impersonation or circumvention.

3.3 **What Dono keeps.** Dono retains the **student-card number** and the extracted details it needs for eligibility, disputes and any institutional referral. **Dono does not retain the student-card image after a successful check** — see the Privacy Notice for timing and safeguards.

3.4 A completed check does **not** mean Dono has verified or guaranteed that any campaign statement is true, that any document is authentic, that any cost is accurate or reasonable, that the Project is viable or lawful in every respect, how the Campaign Owner will behave, that funds will be used as promised, that any institution or society endorses the Campaign, or that the Campaign will succeed. Donors must form their own judgement.

## 4. The Payment Provider's checks and age

4.1 Every Campaign Owner completes the Payment Provider's onboarding for a Stripe Connect Standard connected account before a Campaign can receive Donations. Those identity, KYC and eligibility checks are performed by the Payment Provider, under its own terms, for its own purposes — not for Dono, which does not control them and does not receive the documents collected.

4.2 The Payment Provider's onboarding status is recorded internally and **not** shown publicly: every live Campaign has completed it, so a public label would convey nothing while wrongly implying the Payment Provider has assessed the Campaign.

4.3 **Age.** All Users must be at least 18. Date of birth is declared to Dono; the student card is not relied on as proof of age. Whether the Payment Provider reliably returns a sufficient age signal for every onboarding flow is **[CONFIRM WITH ENGINEERING]**; if it does not, an alternative age-assurance approach is adopted before launch. **[SOLICITOR SIGN-OFF — children's access assessment under the Online Safety Act]**

## 5. Society approval

5.1 A Society Campaign is approved through the relevant society page before it is presented as a society campaign. Society approval means only that the Society approved the Campaign for that presentation. It does **not** mean the institution endorses it, that the Society verified the campaign's contents, that the Society bears legal responsibility, or that the Society is a separate legal person.

5.2 **One approver is sufficient.** The Society registers at least one named Responsible Individual (the Society Representative) who confirms authority to act. A second approver is not required, though Dono may request one for a particular Campaign.

## 6. Institutional endorsement

6.1 "Institutionally endorsed" is used **only** where the Recognised Institution has expressly endorsed that specific Campaign — not because a student used a university email, the institution is listed, a society is associated with it, or a campaign mentions it.

## 7. Statuses shown on the Platform

7.1 Dono shows **plainly worded, factual statements**, not a single combined or unexplained "Verified" badge. Where displayed, and only where accurate, these may include: "Student status manually checked on [date] — campaign claims not checked"; "Society approved"; and "Institutionally endorsed". Each links to this Notice. No status may be presented, and no marketing may suggest, that Dono guarantees a Campaign, its authenticity or its outcome.

7.2 **Evidence status.** Separately, each campaign page shows one of: **"Documents received; line-item amounts matched to budget; documents not authenticated"**; *evidence outstanding*; or *evidence overdue*. The first is a record of an administrative review only — it does not confirm that goods were delivered, that a price was reasonable, that funds were used properly, or that any document is authentic. Receipts themselves are held privately and not shown to Donors.

7.3 Recorded internally and not shown publicly: Payment Provider onboarding status; investigation outcomes; and internal risk classifications.

## 8. Reverification and changes in student status

8.1 Student status is reverified each October, or on the card expiry date if earlier; a check within the preceding three months defers reverification to the next cycle. While reverification is pending and in time, a live Campaign runs normally.

8.2 The **obligations** on a Campaign Owner to remain eligible, to notify interruption, leave or suspension, and to deal with loss of status mid-campaign, are set out in the Student and Society Campaign Terms, not in this Notice.

## 9. Withdrawal of a status; reporting; contact

9.1 Dono may withdraw any status at any time (for example where status lapses, information proves inaccurate, a society withdraws approval, an institution withdraws endorsement, or the Payment Provider closes a Connected Account). Withdrawal is not by itself a finding of wrongdoing. A refusal or withdrawal of a student-status check may be appealed within ten Working Days.

9.2 Any User may report a Campaign they believe inaccurate or in breach, using the reporting tools; Dono investigates under the Terms of Service and the Community Guidelines.

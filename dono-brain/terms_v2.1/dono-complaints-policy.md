## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 This is the Complaints Policy referred to in clause 33.1 of the Terms of Service. It explains how to complain about anything Dono has done or failed to do, how we investigate and decide complaints, and what happens if you remain unhappy with the outcome.

1.2 This policy covers complaints about, among other things: account suspension or restriction (clause 22 of the Terms); campaign removal or an institution report (clause 23); student-status or campaign verification, including delays or outcomes (clauses 6 and 7.3); technical issues with the Platform; moderation decisions, including comment removal (clause 17); and fees (clause 16). It is not limited to these examples — you can complain about any aspect of the Service.

1.3 This policy does **not** cover:

(a) the substantive decision on whether a refund is due, which is decided under the Refund and Dispute Policy, including that policy's own appeal route. This policy covers complaints about the *service* you received during that process — for example, an unreasonable delay — not the refund decision itself.
(b) an appeal against a specific enforcement decision where the Terms already give you a dedicated appeal right — for example, the suspension review at clause 22.2, the campaign-removal appeal at clause 23.5, or the ten-Working-Day moderation appeal at clause 17.2. Use that route to challenge the decision itself. You may still complain under this policy about how that process was handled.

1.4 This Complaints Policy does not replace any specific appeal process provided elsewhere in the Terms, including moderation, suspension or content removal appeals. Where a dedicated appeal process exists, that process should normally be used first.

1.5 Where a matter could go through both a dedicated appeal route and this policy, we won't run duplicate parallel processes on the same point. Tell us which you're using, or we'll ask.

## 2. How to complain

2.1 Send your complaint to **dono.outreach@gmail.com** — the contact address given at clauses 1.2 and 34.2 of the Terms of Service.

2.2 To help us deal with it promptly, please include: your account details; what happened and when; which Campaign or Donation it relates to, if any; the outcome you're looking for; and any supporting evidence (screenshots, correspondence, receipts).

2.3 A complaint is "complete" once we have what we reasonably need to look into it (see clause 2.3 of the Terms of Service). If something's missing, we'll tell you what — normally in a single request.

## 3. Complaint timescales

3.1 The timescales below are operational targets, not contractual guarantees. They give you a sense of what to expect, and give us flexibility where, for example, a complex fraud investigation or legal issue takes longer.

3.2 **Acknowledgement.** We aim to acknowledge your complaint within **2 Working Days** of receipt.

3.3 **Initial assessment.** We normally reach an initial assessment — including whether we need more information, or whether the matter involves a third party such as the Payment Provider or an Institution — within **10 Working Days** of a complete complaint.

3.4 **Substantive response.** We aim to give you a substantive response within **14 Working Days** of a complete complaint. If this takes longer, we'll tell you why and when to expect a response.

3.5 **Internal review.** If you ask for an internal review, we normally complete it within a further **10 Working Days**.

## 4. Investigation and response

4.1 We look at the relevant records, decisions, correspondence and any evidence you or others provide. Where the complaint concerns a decision made by a specific team member, we may ask that person for their account of events.

4.2 If your complaint touches on a live safeguarding, fraud or law-enforcement matter, our response on that part may be limited or delayed to the extent that answering fully would compromise it. We'll tell you this is happening wherever we lawfully can.

4.3 Our substantive response explains what we found, what (if anything) we're doing about it, and how to ask for an internal review if you're not satisfied.

## 5. Internal review

5.1 If you're not satisfied with our substantive response, you must ask for an internal review within **14 days** of receiving it. We may accept a later request in exceptional circumstances, at our discretion.

5.2 The review is carried out by someone who wasn't involved in the original decision, wherever Dono's team size allows. Where the original decision was made by Amrit Kaur Rooprai (the contracting party under the Terms), another member of the Dono team carries out the review in the first instance; Amrit retains final decision-making authority as the operator.

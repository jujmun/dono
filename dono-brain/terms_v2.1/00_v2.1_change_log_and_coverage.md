# Dono legal suite v2.1 — change log & coverage

**Date:** 30 July 2026
**Basis:** the pre-launch counsel review (v0.2, 29 July 2026, 80 issues) and the founder's inline decisions.
**Method:** the v0.2 documents were left untouched; revised versions were written as new `*_v2.1.md` files. Three documents were reclassified as **Notices**. Two new deliverable sets were produced for engineering (`DEV_*`) and operations (`TASKS_*`).

> **Still not for publication.** v2.1 resolves the decided points and rebuilds the drafting, but every document still needs final UK solicitor sign-off and confirmation of the live Stripe configuration and technology stack. Remaining gaps are marked in-document as **[SOLICITOR SIGN-OFF]**, **[CONFIRM WITH STRIPE]**, **[CONFIRM WITH ENGINEERING]** or **[TO BE CALCULATED BY DONO TEAM]**.

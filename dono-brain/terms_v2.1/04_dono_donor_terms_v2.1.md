### 1. About these Terms

1.1 These Donor Terms apply to anyone who makes, or attempts to make, a Donation ("you"). They supplement the Terms of Service. Defined terms have the meanings given there.

1.2 Dono is a technology platform operated by Amrit Kaur Rooprai, a sole trader, of 37 St Giles', Oxford OX1 3LD. Dono hosts Campaigns, checks student status, moderates content and operates an internal refund process. **Dono does not hold campaign funds and does not process card payments.**

1.3 Nothing in these Terms removes or reduces your mandatory statutory rights, your rights against your card issuer or payment provider, or your legal remedies.

### 2. Who can donate; currency

2.1 You must be at least 18 and legally capable of paying. You may be located anywhere, subject to Payment Provider availability, supported cards, sanctions, anti-fraud controls, local restrictions and any restrictions Dono adopts.

2.2 All Campaigns and Donations are in pounds sterling. If your card was issued outside the UK, your card issuer converts the amount at its own rate and may charge its own fees; Dono does not perform the conversion. If you are refunded later, the refund is made in sterling for the amount you gave, and your issuer converts it back at the rate applying on the refund day, so the amount in your currency may differ slightly. This is outside Dono's and the Campaign Owner's control.

### 3. What your Donation is — and is not

3.1 A Donation is a **conditional contribution** to the stated campaign purpose. It is **not**: an investment; a loan; equity or an ownership interest; a payment for goods or services; a guarantee of any outcome; or automatically repayable because you are dissatisfied.

3.2 When your Donation is successfully processed and accepted for the Campaign, **a contract forms directly between you and the Campaign Owner**: you contribute on the published campaign terms, and the Campaign Owner accepts subject to those conditions, including using the funds only for the stated purpose and honouring the published refund triggers. **Dono is not a party to that contract.** Separately, you agree to Dono's Terms for using the Platform, and the Campaign Owner agrees Dono's Terms for using the Platform.

3.3 Donations are not necessarily tax-deductible; Dono issues no charitable tax receipts and claims no Gift Aid. Taxes are your responsibility.

3.4 **Commercial campaigns** are permitted but discouraged and marked on the page. If you donate to one you receive no equity, ownership, repayment right or financial return; any value created belongs to the Campaign Owner. Consider this before donating.

### 4. Who receives your money

4.1 Your Donation is processed by the Payment Provider and paid to the **Recipient's** Connected Account — the person or organisation that connected the Stripe account for the Campaign. Dono receives only its platform fee. For Society Campaigns, the campaign page discloses whether the Recipient is a society/college account or a named representative's account.

### 5. Making a Donation and cancellation information

5.1 Donations are charged when you confirm and are collected even if the target is not met. You must provide correct payment information and be authorised to use the payment method. The Payment Provider's terms and privacy policy also apply.

5.2 **Cancellation of the Dono service element.** Where you choose to pay Dono's fee (by covering fees), you enter a paid distance service contract with Dono for that element. If you ask us to begin providing that service immediately and acknowledge that you may lose any cancellation right once it is fully performed, we will act on that request. This does not affect the separate conditional contribution to the Campaign or your rights against the Campaign Owner. **[SOLICITOR SIGN-OFF — Consumer Contracts Regulations wording]**

### 6. Fees and the fee-cover choice

6.1 **Fee model.** The processing amount replicates the Payment Provider's applicable fee for your transaction (which varies by card type and any currency conversion) **plus 3.5 percentage points, which is Dono's platform fee.** Current worked rates (verify at checkout): standard UK card 5% + 20p; premium UK card 5.4% + 20p; EEA card 6% + 20p; international card 6.75% + 20p; plus 2% only where the Payment Provider actually performs a currency conversion. **[CONFIRM WITH STRIPE]**

6.2 Dono is not registered for VAT, so no VAT is charged on its fee.

6.3 **Your choice.** At checkout you may choose either to **cover the fees**, so your intended campaign amount reaches the Recipient, or to have the **fees deducted** from your donation. Fee cover is **optional, unticked by default, and charged only if you actively select it.** The final button shows the total including any fee cover.

6.4 **Exact total.** Before you confirm, checkout shows the exact total you will be charged, the amount intended for the Campaign, Dono's fee, any optional fee cover and the payment-processing cost. You are never charged more than the confirmed total.

### 7. Receipts

7.1 You receive an email confirming your Donation, showing: the amount; the date; the Campaign; the Recipient who received the money; Dono's platform fee, shown separately; any fee-cover amount you chose; and any currency-conversion cost. It states clearly that it is **not a charitable tax receipt** and that no Gift Aid is claimed. Dono is not the recipient of your Donation.

### 8. Hiding your name

8.1 You may choose to **hide your name**. This hides your name from the public campaign page **and from the Campaign Owner**. It is a single choice.

8.2 Hiding your name has limits and does not make you untraceable, and we explain the re-identification risk when you select it: your donation amount remains publicly displayed, and unique amounts or timing can sometimes identify a donor in a small community; the Payment Provider receives your payment data; and Dono holds your account and donation records. We do not disclose your identity to the Campaign Owner as a dispute-resolution step (clause 11.5).

8.3 Comments always display a name; hidden-name commenting is not available, so if you comment you identify yourself as a supporter. You may change the setting at any time, affecting future display only; it cannot recall anything already seen, screenshotted, cached or archived. A Campaign Owner cannot thank you personally if your name is hidden.

### 9. What you can see about spending

9.1 Campaign Owners submit receipts privately to Dono and publish updates and a Closure Statement. **Receipts are not published or shown to Donors** (they often contain personal information). You instead see a factual **evidence status**: "Documents received; line-item amounts matched to budget; documents not authenticated", *evidence outstanding*, or *evidence overdue*. The first means only that Dono received evidence for the declared expenditure and matched line-item amounts to the budget. It is **not** a statement that money was spent properly, goods were delivered, a price was reasonable, or a document is authentic. Form your own judgement.

### 10. Partial funding, changes and surplus

10.1 If a Campaign does not reach target, the Campaign Owner decides whether the Project can proceed provided the money can still be meaningfully used; otherwise refunds are arranged.

10.2 **Material Changes** follow the three-tier matrix in clause 14 of the Terms of Service. For a *material but equivalent* change you are notified and have **14 days to request a refund**. A *fundamental* change automatically cancels and refunds the Campaign unless you expressly opt in. Affected spending is frozen until the refund window closes.

10.3 **Surplus** that cannot reasonably be used for the stated purpose is refunded in **reverse chronological order** (most recent Donor first), not proportionately, subject to a de minimis threshold set by Dono. This is disclosed at checkout. **[TO BE CALCULATED BY DONO TEAM]**

### 11. Refunds

11.1 **Grounds** include: duplicate payment; payment error; unauthorised payment; campaign cancellation; material misrepresentation; fraud; misuse of funds; failure to provide required evidence; a material unauthorised change; inability to use the money meaningfully; failure to proceed where funds should be returned; or verification failure. Mere dissatisfaction is insufficient.

11.2 **Unauthorised payments — act fast.** If your payment was unauthorised, contact your card issuer or payment provider **at once**: your claim is against them and may require refund by the next business day, subject to statutory exceptions. Dono will preserve evidence and notify the Payment Provider, but Dono's internal process is optional and does not replace or delay your statutory rights.

11.3 **Time limits** (for Dono's voluntary internal process only — these do not shorten your legal rights): an ordinary request within 60 days after the Campaign End Date or after you became aware of the matter, whichever is later; a fraud or deliberate-misrepresentation request up to 12 months after the Donation. Dono may accept late cases where fair, and latent-fraud and legal rights are preserved.

11.4 **How Dono decides.** Dono determines, on the balance of probabilities, whether under the Platform's rules the Campaign Owner is required to issue a refund, considering all relevant evidence. Refunds are granted only where the issue materially affected the donation, and there must be a causal connection between the breach and the refund. Partial refunds may be given. Staff with a conflict of interest do not decide the case. Dono aims to acknowledge within two Working Days, decides within 21 calendar days of a complete request, and may extend once by up to a further 21 days with reasons. External deadlines (card-scheme chargeback windows, statutory limitation periods) continue independently of Dono's process.

11.5 **Identity in a dispute.** A Campaign Owner is told the substance of your allegation and shown the evidence, but is not told your identity where you hid your name. Dono does not disclose your identity to the Campaign Owner as a dispute step; disclosure occurs only where required or permitted by law (clause 25 of the Terms of Service).

11.6 **Appeals** may be brought within ten Working Days and are reviewed by a different member of the Dono team where reasonably practicable, applying conflict-of-interest and recusal rules.

11.7 **Fees on a refund.** Where a refund is due and you were not at fault, **Dono refunds its own platform fee**; where you caused the refund through your own error, Dono's fee is not refunded. On a partial refund, Dono's fee may be refunded proportionately where you were not at fault. Any fee-cover amount you paid is treated the same way as Dono's fee. What the Payment Provider can technically reverse, and the treatment of its processing cost, is confirmed in the Refund and Dispute Policy. **[CONFIRM WITH STRIPE]**

### 12. How an approved refund is paid

12.1 **Dono determines that a refund is required; the Campaign Owner pays it** from their own Stripe account. Dono does not reach into a Campaign Owner's payment account. Dono has sanctions and escalation routes against a Campaign Owner who refuses, but cannot compel a refund to happen or guarantee recovery, and never pays refunds from its own funds. A refund may be delayed or fail where the Connected Account has insufficient funds.

12.2 If a refund you are entitled to is not paid: the responsible account holder remains contractually liable to you; Dono may suspend or ban the user, remove their Campaigns, withhold future payouts not yet released where the flow allows, refer deliberate misuse to their institution, and report suspected fraud; and Dono may assemble evidence to support a chargeback in your favour. You may also pursue chargeback, Payment Provider or legal remedies.

### 13. Chargebacks

13.1 A chargeback is separate from Dono's process and is decided by your card issuer, not Dono. Dono's internal decision does not remove your statutory, payment-provider or legal rights.

13.2 If you are dissatisfied or believe a campaign has breached these Terms, **please contact Dono first** — in most cases we can investigate and, where appropriate, arrange a refund more quickly than a card dispute, we aim to resolve requests within our published timeframe, and we may issue a refund without requiring a chargeback where appropriate. A chargeback should generally be a last resort if the issue cannot be resolved directly with us. **This is guidance, not a restriction.**

13.3 If you choose to raise a chargeback with your card issuer, your issuer decides whether it is available under its rules. **Using a lawful chargeback does not breach these Terms.** Please give accurate information in any refund request or chargeback; knowingly making a false or fraudulent claim may result in suspension of your account and other appropriate action.

13.4 Dono's 21-day decision target may consume part of your card issuer's chargeback window; if timing matters, check your issuer's deadlines. The Connected Account is intended to bear the chargeback and its consequences. **[CONFIRM WITH STRIPE — who submits evidence and who pays dispute fees]**

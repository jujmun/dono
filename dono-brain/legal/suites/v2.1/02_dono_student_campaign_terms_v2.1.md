### 1. About these Terms

1.1 These Student Campaign Terms apply to individual students who submit or operate a Campaign ("you"). They supplement the Terms of Service and take precedence over them where they conflict. Defined terms have the meanings given in the Terms of Service.

1.2 Dono is a trading name operated by Amrit Kaur Rooprai, a sole trader, of 37 St Giles', Oxford OX1 3LD. Dono does not hold campaign funds, does not process card payments, and is not affiliated with any university, college or society unless expressly stated in writing.

1.3 If your Campaign is a Society Campaign, the Society Campaign Terms also apply and take precedence where they conflict. Where you act as a Responsible Individual or as a student Connected Account holder for a Society Campaign, these Terms bind you personally to the extent stated in the Society Campaign Terms (verification, evidence, refund execution, cooperation and continuing obligations).

### 2. Eligibility

2.1 To create and operate a Campaign you must, at creation and throughout: (a) be at least 18; (b) be currently enrolled at a Recognised Institution; (c) be physically studying in the UK; (d) hold and use a valid university email address; (e) provide an acceptable student-card image for one-off checking; (f) complete the Payment Provider's Connected Account onboarding; and (g) remain eligible while the Campaign is active.

2.2 Visiting students and students on placement are eligible if they meet the other requirements. A student on interruption or approved leave of absence remains eligible if they declare they expect to return before the Campaign End Date; a knowingly untrue declaration is a breach and a ground for refusal, suspension, removal and refund. A student suspended by their institution is not eligible while suspended and must tell Dono promptly.

2.3 Not eligible: recent graduates; people who have left; people whose current status cannot be verified; and students within 60 days of their Course End Date. Where a course ends before graduation, the Course End Date governs.

### 3. Student-status checks, reverification and age

3.1 You must submit a valid university email address and an acceptable student-card image. Dono manually reviews the card and current status and aims to complete review within seven Working Days (a target, not a guarantee). **Dono keeps the student-card number but does not retain the card image after a successful check** (see the Privacy Notice).

3.2 The Payment Provider separately conducts identity and KYC onboarding for your Connected Account. Its onboarding status is recorded internally and not shown publicly.

3.3 **Reverification** occurs each October, or on the card expiry date if earlier; a check within the preceding three months defers reverification to the next cycle. While a reverification is pending and in time, your Campaign runs normally.

3.4 You declare your date of birth. Your student card is not relied on as proof of age. **[CONFIRM WITH ENGINEERING — reliability of the age signal in the final implementation]**

3.5 Completion of Dono's checks and the pre-publication validation does not verify every campaign statement, the authenticity of documents, the accuracy of costs, the viability of your Project, your future conduct, institutional endorsement, or completion. Dono does not use "Verified" badges.

### 4. Creating your Campaign

4.1 Your Campaign must include the information listed in clause 7.1 of the Terms of Service, must be accurately represented in both the application and the public page, and must comply with clause 8 of the Terms of Service and the Community Guidelines.

4.2 You must not fundraise on behalf of another individual or run a pass-through campaign. Fundraising on behalf of a Society is governed by the Society Campaign Terms. Commercial ventures are subject to clause 8.3 of the Terms of Service.

### 5. Duration, partial funding and use of funds

5.1 Every Campaign has a Campaign End Date, ordinarily no more than 12 months out and at least 60 days before your Course End Date (extension at Dono's discretion). Donations are charged on confirmation and collected even if the target is not met.

5.2 If your Campaign does not reach target, you decide whether the Project can proceed provided the money can still be meaningfully used for the stated purpose; otherwise you must arrange refunds.

5.3 **Use of funds.** Funds may be used only for the purposes represented to Donors, including approved line items and reasonable directly related expenditure. You may move funds between verified line items within the same stated purpose, update a price where a source is provided, and buy a substitute serving substantially the same purpose at a similar or lower cost.

5.4 **Material Changes** follow the three-tier matrix in clause 14 of the Terms of Service (minor: no consent; material but equivalent: notify donors + 14-day refund window; fundamental: automatic cancellation and refund unless donors opt in). Affected expenditure is frozen until the refund window closes.

### 6. Surplus Funds

6.1 Any amount that cannot reasonably be used for the stated purpose must be refunded. **Surplus is refunded in reverse chronological order** (most recent Donor first), not proportionately. A **de minimis threshold** applies below which a residual amount need not be refunded. **[TO BE CALCULATED BY DONO TEAM]** Fee treatment follows the Refund and Dispute Policy.

### 7. Evidence, updates and closure

7.1 You must declare when you plan to purchase, and submit receipts or other evidence to Dono within **14 days** of each expenditure.

7.2 **Evidence is private** — held by Dono, not published or shown to Donors. Before submitting, redact: full card numbers, security codes and full bank/sort-code details; the names, addresses and contact details of any third party who is not a supplier acting in the course of business; unrelated purchases on the same document; and any special category information (health, religion, sexual orientation, etc.) unless inherent in the campaign purpose. Do not redact supplier names, item descriptions, prices, dates, order references or VAT details, which Dono needs. Dono may itself redact or remove unnecessary personal data from uploads.

7.3 **Updates.** Publish a progress update at least once for every three months of active fundraising, and an outcome update after expenditure.

7.4 **Closure Statement.** Conclude your Campaign with a Closure Statement under clause 10.3 of the Terms of Service (30-day submission deadline, defined minimum contents, Dono's 30-day review, limited rejection grounds, one cure cycle, and deemed acceptance after 30 days, subject to fraud investigations).

7.5 **Evidence status shown to Donors:** "Documents received; line-item amounts matched to budget; documents not authenticated", *evidence outstanding*, or *evidence overdue*. The first is a record of an administrative review only.

### 8. Funded property and disposal

8.1 Unless the campaign page says otherwise, the funded item or output belongs to you and must be used for the stated purpose. It must not be acquired with the intention of immediate resale or personal profit.

8.2 **Disposal.** Prior Dono approval is **not** required to dispose of a funded asset. During the campaign reporting period, you must **notify Dono of any disposal of a material asset purchased using Campaign Funds within 14 days**, stating the reason for the disposal, the sale proceeds (if any), and how those proceeds will be applied. Dono may request supporting information where reasonably necessary. If an item is sold before the campaign purpose is completed, the proceeds must be reapplied to that purpose or refunded; disposal after genuine use for the campaign purpose is permitted.

### 9. Refunds, chargebacks and cooperation

9.1 Donors may seek refunds under the Donor Terms and the Refund and Dispute Policy. You must respond to a refund or information request within five Working Days (a reasonable extension is available on request). Dono aims to decide within 21 calendar days of a complete request, with one possible extension of up to a further 21 days.

9.2 **You execute refunds the Donor is entitled to.** Where Dono determines that a refund is required, you must execute it promptly from your own Stripe dashboard. Dono does not initiate refunds and holds no reserve power to do so. Where the refund is due and the Donor was not at fault, Dono refunds its own platform fee (Refund and Dispute Policy).

9.3 As the Connected Account holder you are the Recipient. Refunds and chargebacks are debited to your Connected Account. **[CONFIRM WITH STRIPE — negative-balance allocation, dispute fees and evidence handling]**

9.4 **Donor identity is not disclosed to you.** A Donor who hid their name is not identified to you. You will be told the substance of any allegation and shown the evidence relied on so you can answer it. Dono does not disclose a Donor's identity to you as a dispute-resolution step; disclosure occurs only where required or permitted by law, following clause 25 of the Terms of Service.

9.5 If a refund the Donor is entitled to cannot be recovered from your Connected Account, you remain contractually liable to the Donor. Dono never pays refunds from its own funds and does not pursue debt collection, but may: suspend or ban you; remove your Campaigns; withhold future payouts not yet released where the payment flow allows **[CONFIRM WITH STRIPE]**; refer deliberate misuse to your institution; and report suspected fraud to the police or another authority. The Donor may pursue chargeback, Payment Provider or legal remedies against you.

### 10. Graduation, departure and change of representative

10.1 Do not start a new Campaign within 60 days of your Course End Date.

10.2 For an **individual** Campaign, the Connected Account and the funds in it belong to you and are not moved between accounts. If you lose student status before the Campaign concludes, the Campaign closes to new Donations and any funds not properly spent must be refunded; you remain the Recipient for Donations already received and remain bound by continuing obligations.

10.3 **Change of authorised representative** applies to Society Campaigns and is governed by clause 6 of the Society Campaign Terms. Any approved change affects only future payment processing and does not alter responsibility for transactions processed before the change.

### 11. Enforcement and your responsibility

11.1 Dono may warn, restrict, suspend, remove or ban under Part 10 of the Terms of Service. Dono cannot necessarily pause a payout already sent or recover money already withdrawn.

11.2 You are responsible for the truth of your Campaign, your authority to run it, lawful conduct, costs and line items, proper use of funds, evidence and redaction, updates and your Closure Statement, the funded property and its disposal, refunds and their execution, chargebacks, cooperation, and claims caused by your fraud, illegality, misrepresentation or IP infringement.

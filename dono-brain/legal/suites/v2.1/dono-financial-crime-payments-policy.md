# Financial Crime & Payments Policy

**Dono** | Last updated: [date] | Owner: Amrit

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out how Dono manages the risk of financial crime — money laundering, terrorist financing, fraud, and sanctions evasion — on the platform. It applies to all staff, contractors, and volunteers involved in reviewing campaigns, handling donor or beneficiary queries, or monitoring platform activity.

Dono is not a payment institution and does not hold a bank-style anti-money laundering programme. Payment processing is carried out by Stripe under a Stripe Connect Standard arrangement, and Stripe's own compliance obligations cover the regulated elements of that processing. This policy covers what Dono itself is responsible for: campaign moderation, verification, prohibited-activity enforcement, and platform-level monitoring.

## 2. Division of responsibility

**Stripe is responsible for:**
- Identity verification (KYC) of campaign creators
- Payment processing, including donor and recipient transactions
- Sanctions screening
- Payment-level fraud detection and chargeback handling

**Dono is responsible for:**
- Reviewing and approving campaigns before they go live
- Verifying that campaign purpose and use of funds are genuine and lawful
- Monitoring the platform for suspicious patterns of activity
- Enforcing this policy, including pausing campaigns and escalating concerns
- Cooperating with Stripe and, where necessary, law enforcement

Dono relies on Stripe's KYC and sanctions checks rather than duplicating them. Dono's own checks are focused on the legitimacy and purpose of campaigns, not on the identity or financial standing of individual users.

## 3. Prohibited activity

The following are prohibited on Dono and will result in campaign removal and, where appropriate, escalation under Section 5:

- Campaigns raising funds for a purpose other than the one stated
- Campaigns created using false, stolen, or borrowed identities
- Structuring donations to avoid verification thresholds
- Using Dono to move funds between parties with no genuine donation purpose
- Any activity that appears designed to launder funds or evade sanctions
- Fraudulent claims about need, circumstance, or beneficiary status

## 4. Detection and escalation

Any member of staff who becomes aware of activity that may fall within Section 3 must escalate it to **Amrit**, who is the designated contact for financial-crime concerns (as with Dono's Online Safety Act reporting).

Escalation should happen as soon as suspicion arises — staff do not need to be certain, and should not attempt to investigate beyond what is needed to form a reasonable suspicion. Staff must not tip off the person concerned.

## 5. Authorised staff actions

Once a concern has been escalated, Amrit (or a staff member acting on Amrit's instruction) is authorised to:

- Pause or remove the campaign from the platform
- Ask Stripe to hold the associated payout
- Restrict or suspend the user's account
- Preserve evidence, including screenshots, messages, campaign content, and any correspondence, before it can be altered or deleted

These actions may be taken singly or together, depending on the severity and nature of the concern. Where a campaign is paused, donors should not be left without an explanation for an unreasonable length of time; a holding message should be provided once it is safe to do so.

## 6. Cooperation with Stripe and law enforcement

Where a concern relates to payment processing, identity verification, or sanctions, Dono will raise it with Stripe directly and follow Stripe's guidance on next steps, including any payout holds or account restrictions Stripe itself requires.

Where a concern may involve criminal activity, Dono will cooperate with law enforcement requests, including preserving relevant records and providing information as required by law. Dono does not attempt to conduct its own criminal investigations.

## 7. Incident records

Amrit maintains a simple incident log (shared spreadsheet) recording, for each escalated concern:

- Date raised and staff member who raised it
- Campaign or account concerned
- Nature of the suspected activity
- Actions taken (pause, payout hold, account restriction, evidence preserved)
- Outcome and any external cooperation (Stripe, law enforcement)

This log is not a regulatory filing; it exists so Dono can show a consistent, documented approach to handling concerns if asked.

## 8. Review

This policy will be reviewed as Dono's scale, payment volume, or Stripe arrangement changes, and at least annually.

# Data Protection Complaints Workflow — Dono

Owner: Amrit (data protection lead)
Last updated: 30 July 2026

This is a distinct process from Dono's Online Safety Act complaints process (which acknowledges within 5 business days and targets a 30-day resolution). Data protection complaints fall under a separate statutory duty in force from 19 June 2026, requiring acknowledgement within 30 days of receipt.

## Process

1. **Receive.** Complaints are submitted via the form below, or by email, or through any other channel a person uses to raise a data protection concern (e.g. "how you're using my data," a subject access request handled badly, a complaint about a data breach).
2. **Acknowledge — within 30 days of receipt.** Confirm receipt and give a brief indication of next steps.
3. **Investigate.** Review the complaint against the relevant processing activity (see ROPA), determine what happened, and identify any remedial action needed.
4. **Communicate the outcome.** Respond to the complainant with the outcome of the investigation and any action taken.
5. **Target resolution within 30 days where reasonably possible.** More complex matters may take longer — if so, keep the complainant informed of progress.
6. **Log.** Record every complaint (date received, nature, outcome, dates of acknowledgement/resolution) for accountability and to spot recurring issues.
7. **Escalate to the ICO where relevant.** If the complaint concerns a possible data breach, follow the incident response plan in parallel.

## Standard response text

> We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days of receiving it. We will investigate it appropriately and respond without undue delay. We aim to resolve most complaints within 30 days where reasonably possible, but more complex matters may take longer. If this happens, we will keep you informed of our progress.

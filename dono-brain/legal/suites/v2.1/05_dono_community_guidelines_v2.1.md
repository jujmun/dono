# Dono Community Guidelines

**Version:** Working draft v2.1 — 30 July 2026
**Status:** DRAFT — NOT FOR PUBLICATION. Applies the counsel review and founder decisions; requires final UK solicitor sign-off including Online Safety Act analysis. Open items marked **[SOLICITOR SIGN-OFF]**.

> **What changed from v0.2:** scope clarified to apply contractually to account holders and people submitting content or using community features; a **Sensitive Personal Information** section added; reporting expanded toward a notice-and-action process; "labels" reframed as factual, not trust badges.

---

### 1. Purpose and scope

1.1 These Community Guidelines apply **contractually to account holders and to anyone who submits content or uses the community features** of Dono (comments, reports, campaign pages, images, documents and usernames). They form part of your agreement with Dono when you take one of those actions.

1.2 Dono is a platform for genuine student fundraising within a university community. These Guidelines keep it honest, respectful, lawful and safe.

### 2. Core principles

2.1 Be honest: campaigns and comments must not mislead. Be respectful: disagreement is fine; abuse is not. Be lawful: nothing may facilitate or promote illegal activity. Be safe: do not endanger, exploit or target others.

### 3. Prohibited content and conduct

3.1 Prohibited anywhere on the Platform: illegal activity or content facilitating it; fraud and scams; misleading fundraising; harassment, threats or abuse; discrimination or hateful content; impersonation; spam; sexual or pornographic content; grossly offensive content; exploitation; dangerous or regulated activity; copyright or trademark infringement; unauthorised use of institutional branding; false claims of affiliation or endorsement; ban evasion; and malicious reporting.

### 4. Sensitive personal information

4.1 Do not publish another person's medical records, diagnosis letters, passport, driving licence, criminal allegations, disciplinary findings, religious information, political opinions or sexual orientation, unless you have the legal right to do so and it is genuinely necessary.

4.2 Do not upload unnecessary sensitive material about yourself either — for example MRI scans, diagnosis letters or identity documents — where the campaign can be made without it.

4.3 **Dono may remove or redact sensitive information even where the Campaign itself remains online,** and may request evidence privately rather than allowing it to be published.

### 5. Campaign integrity and factual statements

5.1 Campaigns must comply with clause 8 of the Terms of Service and the applicable Campaign Terms. Do not create duplicate or deceptive campaigns, fundraise for another individual, run pass-through campaigns, plagiarise, or claim a check, approval or endorsement you do not have.

5.2 Any status shown on a campaign page (for example "Student status manually checked on [date]", "Society approved", or an evidence status) describes a specific, limited, factual check. **Dono does not use "Verified" badges and none of these is a guarantee of a campaign.** Do not misdescribe them.

### 6. Comments

6.1 There is no private messaging; all comments are public and display a name. Hidden-name commenting is not available. You may edit or delete your own comments; the interface shows when a comment was edited. Campaign owners may remove a comment from their own campaign but not edit another user's wording; Dono keeps a private moderation copy and may restore a comment where removal is misused. There is no blocking feature; use reporting instead.

### 7. Reporting and notice-and-action

7.1 Any user may report content or conduct using the on-platform tools or by emailing **dono.outreach@gmail.com**. Reports should identify the content and explain the concern. For illegal content, IP infringement, impersonation or a privacy breach, give enough detail to identify the material and the right claimed. Malicious or knowingly false reporting is itself a breach.

7.2 Dono acknowledges reports, triages them (with an urgent route for genuine illegality or safety risk), decides independently under these Guidelines, the Terms and the law, notifies affected users where appropriate, allows a counter-notice, applies repeat-infringer controls, and preserves records. **[SOLICITOR SIGN-OFF — Online Safety Act reporting/complaints categories and, where in scope, CSEA reporting]**

### 8. Moderation, enforcement and appeals

8.1 Moderation occurs after publication; Dono reviews reported and, where appropriate, proactive content. Dono may warn, remove or hide content, restrict features, restrict a campaign's visibility during investigation, suspend an account, remove a campaign or ban a user, and may refer matters to an institution and report suspected crime.

8.2 You may appeal a moderation decision within **ten Working Days** by emailing **dono.outreach@gmail.com**. Appeals are reviewed by a different member of the Dono team where reasonably practicable, applying conflict-of-interest and recusal rules. This is the same deadline as for internal refund appeals and for appeals against refusal or withdrawal of a student-status check.

### 9. Changes and contact

9.1 Dono may update these Guidelines as the Platform develops and to comply with law, including the Online Safety Act. Material changes are notified under the Terms of Service.

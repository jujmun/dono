### 1. About these Terms and who contracts

1.1 These Society Campaign Terms apply to Campaigns run on behalf of a Society, to the Society, to each Responsible Individual, and to any student whose Connected Account receives Donations for a Society Campaign. They supplement the Terms of Service and the Student Campaign Terms and take precedence for Society Campaigns where they conflict.

1.2 **Contracting parties.**

(a) **Society Representative** means the individual (for example the President or Treasurer) who accepts these Terms on behalf of the Society. That person accepts personally as an authorised representative and warrants that they are authorised under the Society's constitution or governing arrangements to do so. Dono records their full name, email and role.
(b) **Responsible Officers** means the officers authorised under the Society's constitution. Obligations are placed on the Society Representative and, where relevant, the Responsible Officers — **not** on every committee member jointly.
(c) If the Society is incorporated (for example a charitable incorporated organisation or company), the incorporated entity itself may be the contracting party.

1.3 A Society is typically an unincorporated association and is not necessarily a separate legal person. Dono contracts primarily with the Society Representative, who warrants authority to bind the Society under its rules. **[SOLICITOR SIGN-OFF — allocation of obligations and property within unincorporated associations]**

1.4 Society approval, and the appearance of a Society or institution name on a Campaign, does not mean the university, college or institution operates, sponsors or endorses the Campaign or Dono.

### 2. Creating and approving a Society Campaign

2.1 A student may fundraise on behalf of a Society but not on behalf of another individual. A Society Campaign must be approved through the relevant society page before it is presented as a Society Campaign; nothing becomes public automatically, and every Campaign completes the mandatory pre-publication validation.

2.2 **Authority declaration.** On approval the Society Representative confirms: "I confirm that I am authorised under the Society's constitution or governing arrangements to accept these Terms on behalf of the Society." For large campaigns, the Society Representative also confirms the campaign has been approved in accordance with the Society's constitution. Dono does not require the constitution to be supplied every time.

2.3 **One approver is sufficient.** The Society must register at least one named Responsible Individual (the Society Representative), who confirms authority to act. **Approval by a single Responsible Individual is sufficient; a second approver is not required.** Dono may request a second approver for a particular Campaign where it considers this appropriate. (This reflects that many societies do not have two active officers available outside term time.)

2.4 The label "Society approved" means only that the society page approved the Campaign for presentation as a society campaign. It is not an institutional endorsement and not a Dono verification of any campaign statement.

2.5 Society Campaigns must comply with the permitted and prohibited categories in clause 8 of the Terms of Service. Religious-society activities are permitted; alcohol-related expenditure is prohibited, including as part of a society event.

### 3. Payment Recipient and asset ownership

3.1 **Who receives the funds.** Campaign setup asks who will receive the funds. The Recipient is whoever connects the Stripe account, and may be: a Society account; a College/University account; a registered charity; or an authorised Society Representative's account. Donations are paid to the Recipient's Connected Account.

3.2 The Recipient confirms they are authorised to receive the funds, authorised to run the Campaign, and responsible for refunds and chargebacks associated with their Connected Account. One legal regime applies to all Recipients.

3.3 **Record the asset owner.** At setup the Campaign records who will legally own the funded asset (for example: the Society, the College, the University, a charity, a trustee, or named office-holders). Items or outputs funded by a Society Campaign belong to the recorded owner and must be used for the stated purpose; an outgoing officer must not retain funded property personally, and proceeds of any sale remain dedicated to society purposes.

3.4 The ability of a given Society to open the relevant Stripe account is confirmed in practice. **[CONFIRM WITH STRIPE]**

### 4. Responsibilities and refunds

4.1 The Society Representative must: maintain and privately submit evidence within 14 days of expenditure (redacted per clause 7.2 of the Student Campaign Terms); publish required updates and a Closure Statement; notify Material Changes before implementation; respond within five Working Days; and cooperate with refunds and investigations.

4.2 **Execution of refunds.** The Connected Account holder is responsible for refunds and chargebacks and must execute any refund the Donor is entitled to promptly from their own Stripe dashboard. **Dono does not initiate refunds and holds no reserve power to do so.** Where a refund is due and the Donor was not at fault, Dono refunds its own platform fee.

4.3 Receipts and evidence are held privately and not shown to Donors; the campaign page shows only the factual evidence status.

4.4 **Donor identity** is not disclosed to the Recipient or Society as a dispute-resolution step; disclosure occurs only where required or permitted by law (clause 25 of the Terms of Service).

### 5. Resale and disposal

5.1 The disposal rules in clause 8 of the Student Campaign Terms apply: no acquisition intended for immediate resale or personal profit; **no prior approval is required to dispose**, but any disposal of a material asset must be notified to Dono within 14 days with the reason, proceeds and application; pre-completion sale proceeds must be reapplied or refunded; disposal after genuine use is permitted.

### 6. Succession and change of representative

6.1 **Succession planning.** Every Society Campaign must always have a **primary** Society Representative and a **secondary** Responsible Individual (or another authorised committee member), so that a successor is already known if the primary leaves.

6.2 **Managed change of representative.** Dono may approve a change of authorised representative where the Society demonstrates appropriate authority and the incoming representative completes Dono's verification process. **Any change affects only future payment processing and does not alter responsibility for transactions processed before the change.** Personal-account Society Campaigns are **not** automatically non-transferable.

6.3 **Organisation (society/college) accounts.** Where the Society or institution holds the Connected Account, succession is simply a change of authorised representative: the Recipient does not change and no funds move.

6.4 **Representative (personal) accounts.** Where a named student holds the Connected Account, the outgoing representative should initiate a transfer before leaving. A typical flow: (1) the new representative is identity-verified; (2) the committee confirms the appointment; (3) the new representative connects a Stripe account; (4) any remaining balance is handled according to the Payment Provider's capabilities (which may require a payout and a subsequent transfer outside Stripe, depending on Connect restrictions) **[CONFIRM WITH STRIPE]**; (5) future donations go to the new account; and (6) the outgoing representative remains responsible only for transactions processed through their Connected Account before the transfer. Where the Payment Provider does not permit migration, existing donations remain associated with the old Connected Account and future donations are directed to the new Connected Account once the change is approved.

6.5 A change of Responsible Individual, of the Society, or of the Connected Account recipient is a Material Change and follows the Material Change process. Departure does not extinguish a departing individual's accrued obligations, including any personal responsibility as Connected Account holder for pre-transfer transactions.

### 7. Failed recovery and enforcement

7.1 If a refund the Donor is entitled to cannot be recovered from the responsible Connected Account, the account holder remains contractually liable. Dono never pays refunds from its own funds and does not guarantee recovery. Dono may suspend or ban users, remove Campaigns, withhold future payouts not yet released where the flow allows **[CONFIRM WITH STRIPE]**, refer deliberate misuse to the institution, and report suspected fraud to the police or another authority.

7.2 Dono may warn, restrict, suspend, remove or ban the Campaign, the Society's presence, the Responsible Individual or the Connected Account holder under Part 10 of the Terms of Service.

### 8. General

8.1 The liability, indemnity (campaign-owner/society indemnity only), changes, termination, governing-law, complaints and general provisions of the Terms of Service apply.

1.3 Nothing in these Terms removes or reduces your mandatory statutory rights, your rights against your card issuer or payment provider, or your legal remedies.

1.4 **Insurance is limited and is not a guarantee.** Dono holds technology professional-indemnity, cyber/data, cyber-crime, legal-protection and crisis-containment insurance, subject to its policy limits, excesses, conditions and exclusions. The policy is not a reserve or compensation scheme, does not guarantee a refund or payment of a claim, and does not reduce your rights.

### 2. Who can donate; currency

2.1 **You must be 18 or over to donate**, and you must have the legal capacity to enter into the donation agreement. **You do not need a Dono account to donate.** If you would like one, anyone aged 18 or over may open a donor account with **any email address** — you do not need a university address. A donor account lets you follow Campaigns and Societies, see your giving history, and comment where clause 13 permits. You must be using a payment method you are entitled to use.

2.2 **Confirmation at checkout.** Before payment you must confirm: *"I confirm that I am 18 years of age or older and have the legal capacity to enter into this agreement."* **This is a self-certification, not a verified age check**, and we do not pretend otherwise. If we reasonably believe a Donation was made by a person under 18, **we may cancel or refund it**. If you are a parent or guardian and a child in your care made a donation, contact us at clause 14.2 and we will treat it as a refund request under clause 10 and the Refund and Dispute Policy.

2.3 **Where you can be.** You may be located outside the United Kingdom where the Payment Provider supports the payment and where sanctions and other legal restrictions permit. **Dono operates for UK students and has not assessed the law of every country a Donor may be in**; it does not represent that it supports or targets any particular jurisdiction. We may decline a payment where law, sanctions or the Payment Provider requires.

2.4 All Campaigns and Donations are in pounds sterling. If your card was issued outside the UK, your card issuer converts the amount at its own rate and may charge its own fees; Dono does not perform the conversion and receives no part of any issuer charge. A refund is made in sterling for the amount you gave and your issuer converts it back at the rate applying on the refund day, so the amount in your currency may differ slightly.

### 3. What your Donation is — and is not

3.1 A Donation is a **conditional contribution** to the stated campaign purpose. It is **not** an investment, a loan, equity, a payment for goods or services, a guarantee of any outcome, or automatically repayable because you are dissatisfied.

3.2 **How your contract is formed.** By publishing a Campaign the named Campaign Owner makes a **standing offer** to accept eligible contributions on the terms shown on the Campaign page, and **appoints Dono as its disclosed agent** solely to present that offer and communicate acceptance. **Your contract with the named Campaign Owner is formed at the moment the Payment Provider confirms that your payment has succeeded.** Dono then shows you a receipt and sends a confirmation email. **The receipt and the email are evidence of the completed transaction; neither is a condition of the contract, and a failure or delay in delivering either does not affect it.** Dono is not a party to that contract. Separately, you agree Dono's Terms for using the Platform.

3.3 **Who you are contracting with.** Payment cannot proceed unless checkout shows you a **"You're donating to"** panel giving the Campaign Owner's legal name; their legal status; any representative acting for them; the holder of the account receiving the funds; who will own anything purchased with the Donation; and a statement of Dono's role. **The exact information you were shown is stored with your transaction record.** Your confirmation repeats it and records the version of each document that applied.

3.4 **Who may benefit.** A Society Campaign's primary purpose must advance that Society's activities, members or legitimate objectives. Incidental benefit to third parties is permitted. A primarily external-benefit Campaign is permitted during beta only where it is an official Society initiative, approved and controlled by the Society, that directly furthers its charitable, educational, sporting, cultural or community mission. A Campaign may not operate as a pass-through or merely collect for an outside person or organisation. Checkout identifies the Campaign Owner and Recipient so you can decide whether to donate.

3.5 Donations are not tax-deductible. Dono issues no charitable tax receipts and claims no Gift Aid. A charitable-sounding purpose does not mean the Campaign Owner is a charity or that tax relief is available.

3.6 **Not available on Dono:** commercial or entrepreneurial fundraising; any reward, product, service, financial return, repayment, priority access, discount, equity or ownership right; recurring or repeating donations; and matched-funding windows.

### 4. Who receives your money

4.1 Your Donation is processed by the Payment Provider and paid directly into the **Recipient's** Connected Account — the account connected for the Campaign. **Dono never receives or holds your donation.** Dono receives only its fee.

4.2 The Recipient is expected to be the Campaign Owner. For a Society whose legal form does not allow it to hold an account, the Connected Account is held by the Society's Responsible Representative **for the Society and not personally**. Checkout tells you who holds the account in every case.

### 5. Making a Donation

5.1 Donations are charged when you confirm and are collected even if the target is not met. You must provide correct payment information and be authorised to use the payment method. The Payment Provider's terms and privacy policy also apply.

5.2 **Your donation is a one-off payment.** Dono does not offer recurring donations, standing arrangements or subscriptions, and nothing you do at checkout creates a repeating charge.

### 6. Fees

6.1 **The applicable Dono fee is fixed for the Campaign.** A beta/demo Campaign uses **2% + 20p**, displayed as **“Payment processing fee (Dono)”**. A production Campaign uses **5% + 20p**. The fee is the same **whatever card, payment method or country you use**, is Dono's revenue and is charged to the Campaign Owner unless you actively cover it.

6.2 **The Payment Provider's cost is not charged to you.** The Payment Provider charges the Campaign's Connected Account a separate processing cost, which varies by payment method and any currency conversion. **You are never asked to pay it.** Checkout shows it as a deduction from the amount reaching the Campaign, identified as an estimate where the exact figure is not known before you confirm.

6.3 **Fee cover — your choice.** You may choose to add the applicable Dono fee to your payment so that it is not deducted from your contribution. Fee cover is optional, unticked by default and charged only if you actively select it. It is limited to **2% + 20p for a demo Campaign or 5% + 20p for a production Campaign** and never includes the Payment Provider's actual processing cost. Fee cover gives you no additional service and does not make you Dono's customer.

6.4 **The total you will be charged.** Before you confirm, checkout shows: the total; the amount intended for the Campaign; the applicable Dono fee and schedule; any fee cover selected; the estimated Payment Provider cost; and the expected amount reaching the Campaign. **You are never charged more than the total you confirmed.** On a demo Campaign Dono's line is **“Payment processing fee (Dono)”** and Stripe's separate estimate is **“Stripe processing cost (paid by the campaign)”**.

6.5 **Dono is not registered for VAT.** No amount shown to you includes or is described as VAT, and Dono does not issue VAT invoices. Dono is responsible for any VAT on its own fee if it becomes registered.

6.6 Dono may change its fee, but **only for Campaigns created and Donations made after the change takes effect**. Your Donation is never repriced.

### 7. Receipts

7.1 You receive an on-screen receipt and an email **donation confirmation** showing: the amount; the date; the Campaign; the Campaign Owner and the Recipient; Dono's fee; any fee cover you selected; the estimated payment-processing cost; the expected amount reaching the Campaign; and **the version of each document that applied**, either attached or as a permanent link to those exact archived versions. It states clearly that it is **not a charitable tax receipt**, that no Gift Aid is claimed, and that it is **not a VAT invoice**.

### 8. Hiding your name

8.1 You may **hide your name from public display** on Dono. This hides your name from the public campaign page. **It does not hide information the Payment Provider makes available to the Recipient, or to card networks, banks or authorities.**

8.2 **What this does not do.** Your donation amount remains publicly displayed, and a unique amount or timing can sometimes identify a donor in a small community. Dono always holds your name against the payment. Because your payment is charged directly to the Recipient's own Stripe account, **the person or Society operating that account may be able to see the transaction details the Payment Provider shows them, which can include your name — and Dono cannot guarantee otherwise.** We explain this when you select the option. What Dono controls is that **Dono does not disclose your identity to the Campaign Owner as a dispute-resolution step** (clause 10.5), and the Campaign Terms prohibit a Campaign Owner from using payment information to identify, contact or pressure you.

8.3 Comments always display a name, so if you comment you identify yourself as a supporter. You may change the setting at any time, affecting future display only; it cannot recall anything already seen, screenshotted, cached or archived. A Campaign Owner cannot thank you personally on the Platform if your name is hidden.

### 9. What you can see about spending

9.1 Campaign Owners submit receipts privately to Dono and publish updates and a Closure Statement. **Receipts are never published or shown to Donors**, because they routinely contain personal information about other people. You instead see a **neutral lifecycle state** — *funding open*, *funding closed*, *evidence submitted*, *evidence outstanding*, *evidence overdue*, *closed*. It records where the Campaign has reached. **It is not a statement that money was spent properly, that goods were delivered, that a price was reasonable, or that any document is authentic.** Form your own judgement.

9.2 **Dono displays no verification badges, ticks, shields or trust indicators of any kind**, and nothing on the Platform should be read as Dono endorsing, authenticating or guaranteeing a Campaign.

9.3 You are notified when a Campaign you supported closes, and of the outcome of Dono's closure review.

### 10. Refunds

10.1 **Grounds.** The Refund and Dispute Policy governs refunds in full. In summary, a refund may be available where there was: a duplicate payment; a payment error; an unauthorised payment; a donation by a person under 18; cancellation of the Campaign; failure or withdrawal of the Campaign Owner's eligibility; or surplus funds. It may also be available where the Campaign Owner has committed fraud, misused funds, made a material misrepresentation, made an unauthorised material change, failed to provide mandatory evidence after being given a chance to put it right, or cannot use the funds for the stated purpose. **You do not have to prove that any of those things changed your mind about donating.** Mere dissatisfaction with an outcome is not a ground.

10.2 **Unauthorised payments — act fast.** If your payment was unauthorised, contact your card issuer or payment provider **at once**: your claim is against them and may require a refund by the end of the next business day, subject to statutory exceptions. Dono will preserve evidence and notify the Payment Provider, but Dono's internal process is optional and does not replace or delay your statutory rights.

10.3 **Time limits** (for Dono's voluntary internal process only — they do not shorten your legal rights): an ordinary request within **60 days** after the Campaign End Date or after you became aware of the matter, whichever is later; a fraud or deliberate-misrepresentation request up to **12 months** after the Donation. Dono may accept late cases where fair.

10.4 **How Dono decides.** Dono determines on the balance of probabilities whether a refund is required under the Platform's rules, and, if so, the amount. **Proportionality affects how much is refunded, not whether a refund is available.** Dono aims to acknowledge within two Working Days, decides within 21 calendar days of a complete request, and may extend once by up to a further 21 days with reasons. **External deadlines — card-scheme chargeback windows and statutory limitation periods — run independently of Dono's process**, and we will tell you so.

10.5 **Identity in a dispute.** A Campaign Owner is told the substance of your allegation and shown the evidence, but is not told your identity by Dono where you hid your name. Disclosure occurs only where required or permitted by law. Clause 8.2 explains what the Payment Provider may separately show them.

10.6 **Appeals** may be brought within ten Working Days of being notified, under clause 8 of the Community Guidelines.

10.7 **Fees on a refund.** Where a refund is due and you were not at fault, Dono reverses its own fee and returns any fee-cover amount you paid; where you caused the refund through your own error, Dono's fee is not reversed. On a partial refund Dono's fee is reversed proportionately where you were not at fault. The Payment Provider's processing cost is separate and is borne as set out in the Refund and Dispute Policy.

10.8 **If the Campaign does not reach its target.** Not reaching target does not automatically cancel a Project. The Campaign Owner decides whether it can still proceed, provided the money can still be meaningfully used for the stated purpose; otherwise refunds are arranged. **A Campaign cannot be funded beyond its target**: once the target is reached it stops accepting Donations, and if your Donation would take it past the target you are shown the reduced amount before you confirm. If a payment nevertheless completes that takes a Campaign past its target, **the excess is refunded to you in full, with no minimum.**

10.9 **Surplus — money that cannot be used for the stated purpose.** Any amount a Campaign cannot reasonably use for its stated purpose must be refunded. **There is no minimum: every penny of unused money is refundable.** Two things then happen, and you benefit from both:

(a) **Dono refunds surplus automatically in reverse chronological order** — the most recent Donor first, up to the amount they gave, then the next, until the surplus is exhausted. **Surplus is not shared out proportionately**, so an automatic refund is not guaranteed to reach you. This is disclosed at checkout before you pay, and again in the Closure Statement.
(b) **Separately, you may claim your share of unused funds yourself**, at any time, under clause 10.1, whether or not the automatic refunds have reached you. Dono keeps a ledger of every surplus refund so that no amount is refunded twice.

You are notified when a Campaign you supported has surplus, and told how to claim.

### 11. How an approved refund is paid

11.1 **Dono determines that a refund is required. The refund is paid out of the Recipient's own Stripe account** — either because the Recipient executes it, or because **Dono instructs the Payment Provider to reverse the charge under the authority the Campaign Owner and Recipient gave Dono when they accepted the Terms of Service** (clause 13.2). Dono is acting as their agent for that limited purpose. **Dono never receives, holds or pays your money itself.**

11.2 **The honest limits.** A refund can only be paid to the extent the Recipient's account has, or later receives, enough money. **Dono cannot recall funds already paid out and spent, cannot hold or delay a payout, does not guarantee recovery, and never pays refunds from its own funds.** Please take this into account before you donate.

11.3 If a refund you are entitled to is not paid: the responsible account holder remains contractually liable to you; Dono may suspend or ban them, remove their Campaigns, prevent them fundraising on Dono in future, refer deliberate misuse to their Institution, and report suspected fraud; and Dono will assemble the evidence it holds to support a chargeback in your favour. You may also pursue chargeback, Payment Provider or legal remedies.

### 12. Chargebacks

12.1 A chargeback is separate from Dono's process and is decided by your card issuer under card-scheme rules. Neither Dono nor the Payment Provider makes the substantive decision, and Dono cannot prevent or reverse one. Dono's internal decision does not remove your statutory, payment-provider or legal rights.

12.2 **Please contact Dono first** — in most cases we can investigate and, where appropriate, arrange a refund more quickly than a card dispute. A chargeback should generally be a last resort. **This is guidance, not a restriction: using a lawful chargeback does not breach these Terms.** Dono's 21-day decision target may consume part of your issuer's chargeback window; if timing matters, check your issuer's deadlines.

12.3 **One recovery only.** Dono checks for an existing dispute or chargeback before executing any refund. **If you both receive a refund and succeed in a chargeback for the same Donation, the duplicate amount is repayable**, and Dono and the Campaign Owner may take reasonable steps to recover it. Please tell us if a chargeback you raised is resolved in your favour after we have refunded you.

12.4 Give accurate information in any refund request or chargeback. Knowingly making a false or fraudulent claim may result in suspension and other appropriate action.

### 13. Comments and your responsibilities

13.1 **Commenting is restricted.** To post a comment you need an account, must be 18 or over, and must be an **approved member of the Society that owns the Campaign** — you may apply to join a Society, and approval is that Society's decision, not Dono's. Comments are **plain text only** — no links, images or attachments — are published immediately and moderated afterwards, always display a name, and are subject to the Community Guidelines. **Every comment carries a Report control**, and Dono may remove a comment or restrict an account without prior notice where necessary.

13.2 You are responsible for reviewing campaign information before donating, understanding that outcomes are not guaranteed, providing correct payment information, paying any fee-cover amount you select, and using refund and chargeback procedures honestly.

Governed by the law of England and Wales; mandatory consumer rights where you live are unaffected.

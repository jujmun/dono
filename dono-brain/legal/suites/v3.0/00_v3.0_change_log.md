## 1. What v3.0 is

**A clean consolidated suite. Every document states the current position only.**

By v2.3, twelve documents carried an amendment block saying, in effect: *"where the body below conflicts with this block, this block prevails."* That preserved the reasoning and the evidence, which was right. But it produced documents that could not be published or acted on without reading two accounts and working out which won — and in several places the body still described processing that had been removed, corrected only by a block many pages away.

**v3.0 resolves every amendment into the body.** No amendment blocks. No superseded text. No retrospective "changes in v2.2" sections. The drafting history lives in the archive, indexed at `publication-package/SUPERSEDED_VERSION_ARCHIVE.md`.

### 2.4 Material correction — the Children's Code assessment

The 6 August 2026 assessment was written on the premise that **"people under 18 may browse and donate"**. That premise contradicts the settled position, and its consequences ran through the document — it recommended under-18 donation caps, no recurring gifts by under-18s, and excluding declared under-18s from analytics.

**Corrected.** Donors must be 18 or over. An under-18 donation is **not a permitted category to be managed with limits** — it is a rule breach and an objective refund ground. **Caps are deleted and are actively inappropriate**, because a cap implies a smaller under-18 donation is allowed. The approach is **prevent, detect, remedy**. Analytics restrictions now apply to **all** visitors of unknown age, which also removes the under-13 consent problem entirely.

## 3. Nothing is approved

**No document in this suite is signed.** Every approval block is prepared for signature and expressly marked unsigned.

The three largest blocking clusters:

1. **The reporting and moderation system does not exist.** Six of eight Online Safety acceptance tests fail, two partly fail.
2. **The retention and deletion engine does not exist.** No retention or deletion job of any kind runs.
3. **The contract-evidence layer does not exist.** No immutable archive, no version-and-hash acceptance record, no guest linkage, no durable copy.

Plus: **ICO fee registration outstanding**; **CSEA readiness 0 of 12**; **three provider items blocking**.

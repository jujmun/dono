> **The really short version.**
>
> - Anyone can look at Dono. **You have to be 18 to sign up, donate or comment.**
> - If you are just looking around, we collect **almost nothing** about you.
> - We only turn on analytics **if you say yes**. You can say no, and change your mind later.

> - **We never sell your data.** We do not show ads. We do not build a profile of you.
> - You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to fix it, or ask us to delete it.

## 1. What Dono is

Dono is a website where university students raise money for their societies —
sports clubs, music groups, drama, charity projects and so on. People donate,
and the money goes to the society running the campaign.

Dono is run by one person, **Amrit Kaur Rooprai**, in Oxford. Not a big company.

## 2. You can look. You cannot join in until you are 18.

**Anyone of any age can read campaign pages on Dono.** They are public web pages,
like any other website.

**You have to be 18 or over to:**

- create an account
- donate
- write a comment
- run a campaign or act for a society

We know that some people under 18 will see the site anyway. That is why we built
it to be safe by default: **no private messaging, no video streaming, no
disappearing messages, no recommendation feed, and no adverts.** Comments are
plain text only — nobody can put a link or a file in one.

## 3. What we collect if you are just looking

If you are reading pages and not signed in, we collect **almost nothing**.

We do use one thing called **analytics**, which counts how people move around the
site so we can fix what is broken. **It does not run unless you press "Accept" on
the banner.** If you press "Reject", nothing is stored and nothing is sent.

If you do accept, the analytics service gets a random code for your browser —
not your name. We never tell it who you are.

**You can change your mind at any time**, and saying no is exactly as easy as
saying yes.

## 4. What is public on Dono

If you ever do use Dono at 18 or over, it helps to know this in advance:

- **Campaign pages are public.** The campaign name, what it is for, the pictures,
  the target and the total raised can be seen by anyone.
- **Donation amounts are public.** You can hide your *name*, but the *amount* is
  still shown.
- **Comments are public**, with the name on your account.

Hiding your name hides it from Dono's public pages. It does not make you
invisible — the payment company still has your details, and in a small community
an unusual amount can sometimes give someone away. We tell you this at the time,
not afterwards.

## 5. What if you are under 18 and used Dono anyway?

It happens. Here is what we do.

**If you made an account or donated and you are under 18**, tell us and we will
sort it out. **We can cancel or refund the donation**, and we can delete the
account. You will not be in trouble.

**A parent or carer can ask for this too**, and we will treat it as a refund
request.

Email **joindono.team@gmail.com**. Say what happened. You do not need to use
special words or know any law.

## 6. What we do with money information

**Dono never sees your card number.** Card payments are handled by a separate
company called **Stripe**, which is what most websites use. The money goes
straight to the society's own Stripe account — it does not sit with Dono.

Stripe keeps its own records under its own privacy policy.

## 7. Who else sees your information

**We do not sell your data to anyone. Ever.** There is no advertising on Dono.

We do not routinely tell your university, college or students' union anything
about you. There are a few narrow exceptions — mainly if we have to check
somebody really is a student, if we are investigating fraud, if someone might be
at risk, or if the law requires it. When that happens we share **the smallest
amount of information that does the job**, and we normally tell you.

We use a few companies to actually run the website — for hosting it, storing
data, and sending emails. They are only allowed to do what we tell them.

## 8. How long we keep things

Not forever. Different things have different time limits, and they are listed in
full in the Privacy Notice. As a rough guide:

- **Campaign pages** are taken off search engines and archived **two years**
  after the campaign closes.
- **Analytics** is deleted after **12 months**.
- **Records about money** we have to keep for longer, because tax law says so.

## 9. Your rights

These apply to everyone, at any age. You do not need a parent to ask for you.

- **See it** — ask what information we have about you.
- **Fix it** — tell us if something is wrong.
- **Delete it** — ask us to get rid of it. Sometimes we have to keep a little for
  legal reasons, and we will tell you if so.
- **Stop it** — ask us to stop using it in a particular way.
- **Take it** — get a copy to use somewhere else.

**How:** email **joindono.team@gmail.com**. We reply within **one month**. It is
free.

## 10. If you are not happy

**Tell us first** — joindono.team@gmail.com. We would rather know.

If we do not sort it out, you can complain to the **Information Commissioner's
Office (ICO)**, the UK regulator for data protection. Their website is
**ico.org.uk** and they have a helpline. You are allowed to complain to them
even if you have not spoken to us.

## 11. Where to get help

If something on Dono worries you — a campaign that looks wrong, a comment, or
anything that does not feel right — you can **report it** using the report link
on the page, or email us. Reporting is open to **anyone**, whether or not you
have an account, and whatever your age.

If something more serious is going on and you want to talk to somebody outside
Dono, **Childline** (0800 1111, childline.org.uk) is free, and the call does not
show on the phone bill.

## For parents and carers

**Donating on Dono is for adults only.** Account creation, donating and
commenting all require the person to confirm they are 18 or over. For donors that
is a self-declaration at checkout, not a verified age check, and **we do not
claim otherwise**. Anyone creating a campaign goes through the payment provider's
identity check, which does verify age.

**If a child in your care has donated, contact us and we will treat it as a
refund request.** Email **joindono.team@gmail.com**. You do not need to prove
anything unusual to start that conversation.

**Public pages are open to all ages by design**, because campaigns need to be
shareable. Every campaign is reviewed by a person before it goes live, and the
content rules are in the Community Guidelines. We have completed a children's
access assessment, a children's risk assessment under the Online Safety Act 2023,
and an assessment against the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code.

**What we deliberately do not do:** no behavioural advertising, no profiling, no
recommendation feed, no private messaging, no livestreaming, no nudges based on
what someone has donated before, and no "your friends donated" style pressure.
High-privacy settings are the default for everybody, not an option you have to
find.

**The full detail** is in the Privacy Notice, which is written for adults and
covers lawful bases, retention periods, international transfers and processors.

**To raise a concern about a child's data**, email joindono.team@gmail.com. If we
do not resolve it, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) regulates
us.

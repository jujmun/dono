### 1. About these Terms

1.1 These Student Campaign Terms apply to individual students who submit or operate a Campaign ("you").

1.2 Dono is a trading name of Amrit Kaur Rooprai, a sole trader, of 37 St Giles', Oxford OX1 3LD. Dono does not receive, hold, safeguard or control campaign funds, holds no payment account, does not process card payments, and is not affiliated with any university, college or society unless expressly stated in writing.

1.3 If your Campaign is a Society Campaign, the Society Campaign Terms also apply and govern the Society relationship.

1.3A **Beta and future release.** Individual Campaigns are not available during the Society-only beta, and these Terms are not presented as operative individual-Campaign terms during beta. When individual Campaigns are released, you may create one in your own name and receive Donations into your own Connected Account. These Terms continue to govern the personal conduct obligations incorporated into the Society Campaign Terms.

1.4 **You are ordinarily a consumer.** Running a Campaign to fund your studies, research, travel or a student project is not a trade or business. Unless you are genuinely acting for the purposes of a trade, business, craft or profession, you are a consumer: **there is no monetary cap on Dono's liability to you, and you are subject to no indemnity** (Terms of Service clauses 27–28).

### 2. Eligibility

2.1 To create and operate a Campaign you must, at creation and throughout: (a) be at least 18 under the verified-DOB rule in clause 3.4; (b) be currently enrolled at a Recognised Institution; (c) hold, use and verify control of a valid university email address; (d) provide a valid UK address and complete the Payment Provider's UK Connected Account onboarding in your own name; and (e) remain eligible while the Campaign is active. Physical presence in the UK is not required.

2.2 A student remains eligible while enrolled during a year abroad, placement, field trip or other temporary period outside the UK, provided they continue to meet clause 2.1, including the UK address and Payment Provider requirements. A student on interruption or approved leave remains eligible only if the Institution continues to treat them as enrolled and they expect to return before the Campaign End Date. A suspended student is not eligible while suspended.

2.3 Not eligible: recent graduates; people who have left; people whose eligibility cannot be confirmed; and students within 60 days of their Course End Date. Where a course ends before graduation, the Course End Date governs.

### 3. Eligibility checks and age

3.1 **Two checks, by two different people.**

(a) **Dono checks your student status** by confirming you control an email address at a Recognised Institution — a one-time code or link sent to that address and returned. This is the only check Dono performs itself. **Dono does not ask for, collect, process or store a student card, a student-card image or a student number**, and does not contact your Institution.
(b) **The Payment Provider checks your identity**, using a **government-issued identity document and a face scan**. **You submit those to the Payment Provider, which holds them as an independent data controller. Dono never receives, sees or stores your identity document or your face scan.** Dono receives and records only whether the check passed and the **verified name and date of birth** it returns, visible to Dono administrators only.

**You cannot create, publish or receive Donations on a Campaign while the identity check is pending or has failed.**

3.2 The Payment Provider separately conducts identity and KYC onboarding for your Connected Account. Its onboarding status is recorded internally and is not shown publicly. **Dono does not receive the identity documents the Payment Provider collects.**

3.3 **Reverification** occurs each October and at Campaign creation; a check within the preceding three months defers reverification to the next cycle. While a reverification is pending and in time, your Campaign runs normally. Where your university email address stops working, or Dono has reason to doubt your eligibility, Dono may pause the Campaign pending a check.

3.4 **Age.** You must be at least 18 to hold an account and create a Campaign. Your declared date of birth is a preliminary gate. **The date of birth returned by the Payment Provider's government-document identity check is the final creator age gate.** A missing, inconsistent or under-18 result blocks creation, publication and receipt of Donations. You may request review of an apparent error, but Dono does not manually override an under-18 result without corrected Payment Provider data. Donor age remains self-certified.

3.5 Completing Dono's checks and the pre-publication review does not verify any campaign statement, the authenticity of any document, the accuracy of any cost, the viability of your Project, your future conduct, institutional endorsement, or completion. **Dono displays no verification badges or trust indicators of any kind.**

### 4. Creating your Campaign

4.1 Your Campaign must include everything listed in clause 7.1 of the Terms of Service, including the **Ownership Statement**, must be accurately represented in both the application and the public page, and must comply with clause 8 of the Terms of Service and the Community Guidelines.

4.2 **The Campaign must be for you.** Funds must be raised for your own activity as a student, and the funded property or output must belong to you under the Ownership Statement. You may **not**:

(a) fundraise for another individual, household, group or organisation;
(b) fundraise for a charity, a charitable cause, or a purpose that is charitable in substance rather than funding your own activity;
(c) run a campaign whose primary purpose is to benefit the general public rather than you;
(d) run a pass-through campaign; or
(e) run a campaign for the purposes of a trade, business, craft, profession or entrepreneurial venture.

Clause 8.4 of the Terms of Service gives worked examples. Fundraising for a Society is governed by the Society Campaign Terms.

4.3 **At creation you must declare** whether any charity, community organisation or third party is involved in or will benefit from the Campaign, who will receive the funds, who will own anything purchased, and whether the Campaign is run for the purposes of a trade or business. A false declaration is a breach and a ground for removal and refund.

4.4 **Recurring donations and matched-funding windows are not available**, and your Campaign may not offer or advertise either.

4.5 **Video and links.** Any campaign video must be uploaded directly to the Platform and is reviewed in full before publication and again after any change. External links must use HTTPS, are stored and checked, are re-reviewed whenever edited, and may be disabled at any time.

### 5. Duration, partial funding and use of funds

5.1 Every Campaign has a Campaign End Date, ordinarily no more than 12 months out and at least 60 days before your Course End Date (extension at Dono's discretion). Donations are charged on confirmation and collected even if the target is not met. **A Campaign cannot be funded beyond its target**: once the target is reached the Campaign stops accepting Donations. If a payment nevertheless completes that takes the Campaign past its target, **the excess is refunded in full, with no de minimis** (clause 14.5(c) of the Terms of Service).

5.2 If your Campaign does not reach target, you decide whether the Project can proceed provided the money can still be meaningfully used for the stated purpose; otherwise you must arrange refunds.

5.3 **Use of funds.** Funds may be used only for the purposes represented to Donors, including approved line items and reasonable directly related expenditure. You may move funds between line items within the same stated purpose, update a price where you provide a source, and buy a substitute serving substantially the same purpose at a similar or lower cost.

5.4 **Money not used for the stated purpose must be refunded in full.** If you apply funds to anything outside the stated purpose, or to a purpose that is not strongly related to it, **the whole of the affected amount is refundable. There is no de minimis and no allowance for small deviations.** Ask for the amount you actually need, first time.

5.5 **Material Changes** follow clause 14.4 of the Terms of Service. You must **submit the change to Dono before implementing it**; new donations are suspended; affected Donors are notified individually and have 14 days to request a refund; fundamental changes require cancellation and refunds unless Donors opt in.

5.6 **You must not spend the affected funds** from the moment a Material Change arises until the refund window closes. Dono cannot freeze your funds or your payouts and does not claim to be able to. **Breaching this obligation may lead to suspension or removal from the Platform, a referral to your Institution, a report to the police or another authority, and legal action by affected Donors.** Where the money has already been spent, a refund depends on what you still hold or can pay. A Material Change may not be made after funds have been spent in order to regularise that spending.

### 6. Surplus Funds

6.1 Any amount that cannot reasonably be used for the stated purpose must be refunded. **There is no de minimis threshold: every penny of unused money is refundable.**

6.2 Dono refunds surplus automatically in **reverse chronological order** — most recent Donor first — and, independently of that, **any Donor may claim a refund of their share of unused funds** under the Refund and Dispute Policy. Dono keeps a ledger so that no amount is refunded twice. You must declare the treatment of surplus in your Closure Statement. Fee treatment follows the Refund and Dispute Policy.

### 7. Evidence, updates and closure

7.1 You must declare when you plan to purchase and submit receipts or other evidence to Dono within **14 days** of each expenditure.

7.2 **Evidence is private** — held by Dono, never published or shown to Donors. **Before uploading, you must redact personal data that is not needed to verify the purchase**: the names, addresses, email addresses, telephone numbers and delivery addresses of individuals; payment-card details, security codes and full bank or sort-code details; unrelated purchases on the same document; and any special category information (health, religion, sexual orientation and similar) unless it is inherent in the campaign purpose. **Do not redact** supplier names, item descriptions, prices, dates, order references or VAT details, which Dono needs. Guidance is shown before every upload. **A receipt containing unnecessary personal data may be rejected**, and rejected uploads are quarantined and automatically deleted shortly afterwards; you will be asked to re-upload a properly redacted version. Dono may itself redact or remove unnecessary personal data.

7.3 **Updates.** Publish a progress update at least once for every three months of active fundraising, and an outcome update after expenditure.

7.4 **Closure Statement.** Conclude your Campaign with a Closure Statement under clause 10.3 of the Terms of Service: 30-day submission deadline with reminders, defined minimum contents, Dono's 30-day review against a reviewer checklist, limited rejection grounds, **one cure cycle**, and **administrative closure** if Dono does not decide in time. **Administrative closure is labelled as procedural and is not a finding that you complied** — it records only that Dono did not complete its review in time, and it does not prejudice a Donor's rights or any later review. You may appeal a rejection within ten Working Days.

7.5 **What Donors see** is a neutral lifecycle state only — *funding open*, *funding closed*, *evidence submitted*, *evidence outstanding*, *evidence overdue*, *closed*. It is a record of where the Campaign has reached, not a statement that money was spent properly or that any document is genuine.

### 8. Funded property and disposal

8.1 Unless the Ownership Statement says otherwise, the funded item or output belongs to you and must be used for the stated purpose. It must not be acquired with the intention of immediate resale or personal profit.

8.2 **Disposal.** Prior Dono approval is **not** required. During the campaign reporting period you must **notify Dono of any disposal of a material asset purchased with Campaign funds within 14 days**, stating the reason, the proceeds and how they will be applied. If an item is sold before the campaign purpose is completed, the proceeds must be reapplied to that purpose or refunded. Disposal after genuine use for the campaign purpose is permitted.

### 9. Refunds, chargebacks and cooperation

9.1 Donors may seek refunds under the Donor Terms and the Refund and Dispute Policy. You must respond to a refund or information request within **ten Working Days**, extendable on reasonable request where you are unwell, on holiday, sitting examinations or otherwise genuinely unable to respond. Dono aims to decide within 21 calendar days of a complete request, with one possible extension of up to a further 21 days.

9.2 **Refund execution and Dono's mandate.** Where Dono determines that a refund is due you must execute it promptly from your own Stripe dashboard. **In addition, by accepting these Terms you irrevocably authorise Dono, as your agent for that limited purpose only, to instruct the Payment Provider to reverse the charge, in whole or in part, from your Connected Account** (Terms of Service clause 13.2). Dono will normally do so only after notifying you and giving you the opportunity to respond, except where a Donation must be reversed urgently to prevent fraud, unauthorised payment or harm. **You may appeal Dono's determination within ten Working Days of being notified**, but the refund may already have been executed and Dono does not undertake to reverse it. Dono never takes money for itself under this mandate and never holds your funds; where a refund is due and the Donor was not at fault, Dono separately reverses its own fee.

9.3 **You must maintain sufficient funds** in your Connected Account to meet any refund that becomes due, and must not withdraw funds in order to defeat a pending refund request. A refund executed against an insufficient balance may create a negative balance for which **you** are responsible to the Payment Provider.

9.4 As the Connected Account holder you are the Recipient. Refunds and chargebacks are debited to your Connected Account, and the Payment Provider charges your account its processing and dispute fees. **You own any dispute and are responsible for submitting evidence to the Payment Provider.** Dono will notify you as soon as it becomes aware that a dispute has been opened, alert you before the card-network deadline, and provide the records it holds. Where a negative balance cannot be recovered from your account, the Payment Provider is the losses collector under Dono's configuration.

9.5 **No double recovery.** If a Donor both receives a refund and succeeds in a chargeback for the same Donation, the duplicate amount is recoverable. You must tell Dono promptly if you become aware of a duplicate recovery, and Dono may take reasonable steps to recover it.

9.6 **Donor identity is not disclosed to you by Dono.** You will be told the substance of any allegation and shown the evidence relied on so you can answer it, but a Donor who hid their name is not identified to you by Dono; disclosure occurs only where required or permitted by law (Terms of Service clause 25). **Separately**, because Donations are charged directly to your Connected Account, the Payment Provider may show you transaction details including a Donor's name whatever their display preference on Dono. That is outside Dono's control, and **you must not use it to identify, contact or pressure a Donor who chose to hide their name** — doing so is a breach of these Terms.

9.7 If a refund a Donor is entitled to cannot be recovered from your Connected Account you remain contractually liable to the Donor. **Dono never pays refunds from its own funds and does not pursue debt collection**, but may suspend or ban you, remove your Campaigns, prevent you fundraising on Dono in future, refer deliberate misuse to your Institution under the Institutional Referral Protocol, and report suspected fraud to the police or another authority. **Dono cannot withhold or delay a payout from your Connected Account.** The Donor may pursue chargeback, Payment Provider or legal remedies against you.

### 10. Graduation and departure

10.1 Do not start a new Campaign within 60 days of your Course End Date.

10.2 The Connected Account and the funds in it belong to you and are not moved between accounts. If you lose eligibility before the Campaign concludes, the Campaign closes to new Donations and any funds not properly spent must be refunded; you remain the Recipient for Donations already received and remain bound by continuing obligations.

### 11. Enforcement and your responsibility

11.1 Dono may warn, restrict, suspend, remove or ban under Part 9 of the Terms of Service. Dono cannot pause a payout, recover money already withdrawn, or reverse a payment other than through the refund mandate in clause 9.2.

11.2 You are responsible for: the truth of your Campaign; your authority to run it; lawful conduct; costs and line items; proper use of funds; evidence and redaction; updates and your Closure Statement; the funded property and its disposal; refunds and their execution; chargebacks and dispute evidence; cooperation; and claims caused by your fraud, illegality, misrepresentation or intellectual-property infringement.

11.3 **Fees, receipts and tax records.** **You are Dono's customer for the platform service in every case**, whether or not a Donor covers Dono's fee. Dono's fee is **5% + 20p** and is separate from the Payment Provider's processing cost, which is charged to your Connected Account. Donations are paid to your Connected Account and are not Dono's revenue; Dono records only its own fee as revenue. **Dono is not VAT registered and issues no VAT invoices**; it provides a statement of its fee and any reversal. The treatment of your receipts for VAT, income tax or any other tax depends on your own activities and circumstances — Dono does not advise on it and does not transfer its own tax obligations to you. Retain your Connected Account statements, donation confirmations, refunds, disputes, fee records and evidence of expenditure.

### 12. General

12.1 The liability, changes, termination, governing-law, complaints and general provisions of the Terms of Service apply. **Because you are ordinarily a consumer, clause 27.3(a) applies to you — reasonably foreseeable loss, no monetary cap — and no indemnity applies.** If you are genuinely running your Campaign for the purposes of a trade or business, clauses 27.3(c) and 28.2 apply instead. Governed by the law of England and Wales.

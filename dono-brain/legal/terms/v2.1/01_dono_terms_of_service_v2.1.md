### 12. Nature of a Donation

12.1 A Donation is a **conditional contribution** to the stated campaign purpose — not an unconditional gift, an investment, a loan, equity, a payment for goods or services, or a guarantee of any outcome. Three separate relationships arise:

(a) **Donor and Campaign Owner:** the Donor contributes on the published campaign terms; the Campaign Owner accepts the contribution subject to those conditions, including using the funds only for the stated purpose and honouring the published refund triggers. **A contract forms between the Donor and the Campaign Owner when the Donation is successfully processed and accepted for the Campaign.** Dono is not a party to that contract.
(b) **Donor and Dono:** the Donor separately agrees to these Terms and the Donor Terms for use of the Platform.
(c) **Campaign Owner and Dono:** the Campaign Terms govern the Campaign Owner's use of the Platform and obligations to Dono.

12.2 A Donation is not automatically repayable because a Donor is dissatisfied with an outcome, and is not necessarily tax-deductible.

### 13. Refunds (overview)

13.1 Dono operates an internal refund and appeal process (Donor Terms and Refund and Dispute Policy). **Dono determines whether, under the Platform's rules, a Campaign Owner is required to issue a refund.** Dono is not awarding damages and does not itself pay refunds from its own funds. The Campaign Owner executes any refund the Donor is entitled to. Where a refund is due and the Donor was not at fault, **Dono refunds its own platform fee** as set out in the Refund and Dispute Policy.

13.2 Nothing in these Terms removes a Donor's statutory, payment-provider or chargeback rights.

### 14. Use of funds; changes; surplus

14.1 **Use of funds.** Campaign funds may be used only for the purposes represented to Donors, including approved line items and reasonable directly related expenditure. Funds may be moved between verified line items within the same stated purpose.

14.2 **Partial funding.** If a Campaign does not meet its target, the Campaign Owner decides whether the Project can proceed with the amount raised, provided it can still be meaningfully used for the stated purpose; otherwise refunds must be arranged.

14.3 **Material Change — definition.** A **Material Change** is any change that a reasonable donor would consider important when deciding whether to make the donation.

14.4 **Material Change — three categories and remedy.**

(a) **Minor changes** — no donor consent required (for example: supplier changes, a different manufacturer, colour changes, small budget reallocations, timetable changes).
(b) **Material but equivalent changes** — Dono is notified, donors are notified, and donors have **14 days to request a refund** (for example: different equipment, a different contractor, significant budget reallocations, a different venue).
(c) **Fundamental changes** — the Campaign is automatically cancelled and refunded unless donors expressly opt in (for example: changing the purpose entirely, changing the beneficiary, changing the organisation, or using funds for operating expenses instead of the advertised project).

Affected expenditure is **frozen until the refund window closes** so that donors entitled to refunds are not defeated by immediate spending.

14.5 **Surplus Funds.** Any amount that cannot reasonably be used for the stated purpose must be refunded. Because proportionate micro-refunds are often uneconomic, **surplus is refunded in reverse chronological order** — the most recent Donor first, up to the amount they donated, then the next, until the surplus is exhausted. Surplus is not distributed proportionately. A **de minimis threshold** applies below which a residual amount need not be refunded (for example, where a campaign for £1,000 raises £1,001, the remaining £1 need not be refunded). **[TO BE CALCULATED BY DONO TEAM — set the de minimis figure(s) accounting for the 20p fixed component and Stripe per-transaction costs, and disclose it at checkout and closure.]**

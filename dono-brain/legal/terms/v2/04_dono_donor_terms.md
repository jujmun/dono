### 1. About these Terms

1.1 These Donor Terms apply to anyone who makes, or attempts to make, a Donation on Dono ("you"). They supplement the Dono Terms of Service, which continue to apply. Defined terms have the meanings given in the Terms of Service.

1.2 Dono is a trading name operated by Amrit Kaur Rooprai, a sole trader, of 37 St Giles', Oxford OX1 3LD. Dono is a technology platform: it hosts Campaigns, conducts limited student-status checks, moderates content and operates an internal refund process. Dono does not hold campaign funds and does not itself process card payments.

1.3 Nothing in these Terms removes or reduces your mandatory statutory rights, your rights against your card issuer or payment provider, or your legal remedies.

### 2. Who can donate

2.1 You must be at least 18 and legally capable of making the payment. You may be located anywhere, subject to Payment Provider availability, supported cards, sanctions, anti-fraud controls, local legal restrictions and any restrictions Dono adopts.

2.2 **Currency.** All Campaigns and Donations on Dono are in pounds sterling (GBP). If your card was issued outside the United Kingdom you can still donate; your card issuer converts the amount at its own rate and may charge you its own fees. Dono does not perform the conversion and has no control over the rate you receive.

2.3 **A note about refunds and exchange rates.** If you are refunded some time after donating, the refund is made in pounds sterling for the amount you gave. Because your card issuer converts it back at the rate applying on the day of the refund, the amount you receive in your own currency may be slightly more or slightly less than you originally paid. This is outside Dono's control and outside the Campaign Owner's control.

### 3. What your Donation is — and is not

3.1 A Donation is a donation or gratuitous contribution to the stated campaign purpose. It is **not**:

(a) an investment;
(b) a loan;
(c) equity or an ownership interest;
(d) a payment for goods or services;
(e) a guarantee of any outcome; or
(f) automatically repayable because you are dissatisfied with the outcome.

3.2 Donations are not necessarily tax-deductible. Dono does not issue charitable tax receipts and Gift Aid is not claimed on Donations. Taxes are your responsibility.

3.3 Campaign Owners are students or societies, not Dono. Dono does not guarantee the identity of any user beyond its limited published checks, or the authenticity, accuracy, viability, completion or outcome of any Campaign.

3.4 **Commercial campaigns.** A small number of Campaigns relate to commercial ventures. These are permitted but discouraged, and are marked on the campaign page. If you donate to one, you receive no equity, ownership, repayment right or financial return of any kind, while any value the venture creates belongs to the Campaign Owner. Consider that carefully before donating.

### 4. Who receives your money

4.1 Your Donation is processed by the Payment Provider (Stripe) and paid to the Connected Account of the Campaign Owner. The Connected Account holder is the Merchant of Record. Dono receives only its platform fee.

4.2 For Society Campaigns, the campaign page discloses whether the legal recipient is a society account or a named student's account.

### 5. Making a Donation

5.1 Donations are charged immediately and are collected even if the Campaign's funding target is not met.

5.2 Available payment methods depend on the Payment Provider. You must provide correct payment information and must be authorised to use the payment method.

5.3 The Payment Provider's terms and privacy policy also apply to your payment.

### 6. Fees and the fee-cover choice

6.1 **Dono's platform fee is 5% of your Donation plus 20 pence.** On smaller Donations the fixed 20 pence is a larger share of the total — on a £5 Donation the fee is 45 pence, which is 9%. The exact amount is always shown before you confirm.

6.2 **VAT.** Dono is not currently registered for VAT, so no VAT is charged on the platform fee.

6.3 The Payment Provider charges its own processing fees, which vary by card type, country and currency and may change. No single processing price applies to every payment.

6.4 **Non-UK cards.** If you use a card issued outside the United Kingdom, the Payment Provider charges an additional amount, currently 2% of the Donation. This is passed through to you and shown as a **separate line** at checkout. It is not a Dono fee and Dono does not keep it.

6.5 At checkout you may choose to:

(a) cover Dono's fee and the estimated Payment Provider fees, so that your intended campaign amount reaches the recipient; or
(b) decline, in which case fees are deducted from the amount the Campaign receives.

6.6 Checkout shows separately: your intended campaign donation; the Dono platform fee; the estimated Payment Provider processing fee; the non-UK card cost where it applies; the total charged; and the expected amount reaching the Campaign.

### 7. Receipts

7.1 You will receive an email confirming your Donation. The receipt shows: the amount you paid; the date; the Campaign; the name of the Merchant of Record, being the Campaign Owner or Society account that received the money; the Dono platform fee, shown separately; any fee-cover amount you chose to pay; and any non-UK card cost.

7.2 The receipt states clearly that it is **not a charitable tax receipt** and that no Gift Aid is claimed.

7.3 The receipt is issued through Dono and carries Dono branding, but it records a payment made to the Campaign Owner as Merchant of Record, and identifies them as the recipient. Dono is not the recipient of your Donation.

### 8. Anonymous donations

8.1 You may choose to make your Donation **anonymous**. This hides your name from the public campaign page **and from the Campaign Owner**. It is a single choice: there is no separate setting for public display and for the Campaign Owner.

8.2 Anonymity has limits, and does not mean you are untraceable:

(a) your donation amount remains publicly displayed;
(b) the Payment Provider receives and processes your payment data;
(c) Dono holds your account and donation records; and
(d) in a refund dispute, Dono may disclose your identity to the Campaign Owner where it is genuinely necessary for them to answer an allegation you have made, and will record its reasons.

8.3 **Comments always display a name.** If you donate anonymously and then post a comment on that Campaign, you will identify yourself as a supporter. Anonymous commenting is not available.

8.4 You may change your anonymity setting at any time, in either direction. Changing it affects future display only. It cannot recall anything a Campaign Owner has already seen, or anything already screenshotted, cached or archived.

8.5 One consequence worth knowing: a Campaign Owner cannot thank you personally for an anonymous Donation, because they do not know who you are.

### 9. What you can see about how money was spent

9.1 Campaign Owners must submit receipts and evidence of expenditure to Dono, and must publish progress updates and a final Closure Statement.

9.2 **Receipts are not published and are not shown to Donors.** They often contain personal information about the Campaign Owner and third parties, and publishing them would create privacy risks out of proportion to the benefit.

9.3 What you see instead is a factual **evidence status** on the campaign page: *evidence of expenditure received and reviewed*, *evidence outstanding*, or *evidence overdue*.

9.4 "Evidence of expenditure received and reviewed" means only that Dono has received evidence for the expenditure the Campaign Owner declared, and that an administrator has reviewed it against the Campaign's stated budget. It is **not** a statement by Dono that the money was spent properly, that goods were delivered, that a price was reasonable, or that any document is authentic. You should still form your own judgement about a Campaign.

9.5 You also see the Campaign Owner's published updates and their Closure Statement.

### 10. Partial funding, changes and surplus

10.1 If a Campaign does not reach its target, the Campaign Owner decides whether the Project can proceed. If the money raised can still be meaningfully used for the stated campaign purpose, the Project may proceed and your Donation is retained. If it cannot be meaningfully used, the Campaign Owner must arrange refunds.

10.2 Campaign Owners may move funds between verified line items within the same stated campaign purpose and make limited changes under the Campaign Terms. Material Changes require Dono review, a campaign-page update and donor notice where appropriate, and may give rise to refund rights.

10.3 **Surplus Funds.** Any amount that cannot reasonably be used for the stated campaign purpose must be refunded. Surplus is refunded in **reverse chronological order of donation**: the most recent Donor is refunded first, up to the amount they donated, then the next most recent, until the surplus is exhausted. Surplus is **not** distributed proportionately across all Donors, and no Donor is guaranteed a proportionate surplus refund. There is currently no de minimis threshold. **[SUBJECT TO STRIPE CONFIGURATION — fee mechanics]**

### 11. Refunds

11.1 **Grounds.** You may request a refund through Dono's internal process on grounds including: duplicate payment; payment error; unauthorised payment; campaign cancellation; material misrepresentation; fraud; misuse of funds; failure to provide required evidence; a material unauthorised change; inability to use the money meaningfully for the stated purpose; failure to proceed where funds should therefore be returned; or verification failure. Mere dissatisfaction with the outcome is insufficient.

11.2 **Time limits.** An ordinary refund request must be made within 60 days after the Campaign End Date, or within 60 days after you became aware of the alleged breach, where later. A request based on fraud or deliberate material misrepresentation may be made up to 12 months after the Donation.

11.3 **Process.** Either party may submit evidence, including receipts, photographs, purchase records, financial information, campaign updates, communications, and links or pricing sources. The Campaign Owner has five working days to respond to a request. **Dono aims to decide within 21 calendar days** of receiving a complete request; complex or contested cases may take longer, and Dono will say so. Dono may decide on no refund, a partial refund or a full refund.

11.4 **Appeals.** You may appeal Dono's initial decision within **ten working days**. The appeal is reviewed by a different administrator where reasonably practicable.

11.5 **Fee treatment.** In broad terms: if a campaign cannot proceed because it did not raise enough, Dono refunds its own fee but not the Payment Provider's fee; if the Campaign Owner caused the refund through mistake, breach or misrepresentation, you should receive Dono's fee back and the Campaign Owner bears the economic cost where technically possible; if you caused the refund through your own error, you bear the non-refundable Dono fee; for partial refunds, Dono's fee may be refunded proportionately where you were not at fault. The published Refund and Dispute Policy will state exactly what you receive, who bears the cost, and what the Payment Provider can technically reverse. **[SUBJECT TO STRIPE CONFIGURATION]**

### 12. How an approved refund is actually paid — important

12.1 **Dono decides that a refund is owed. The Campaign Owner pays it.** An approved refund is executed by the Campaign Owner from their own Stripe account. Dono does not reach into a Campaign Owner's payment account and move their money.

12.2 This matters to you, so it is worth stating plainly: Dono has sanctions and escalation routes against a Campaign Owner who refuses, but **Dono cannot compel a refund to happen**.

12.3 A refund may be delayed, or may fail, where the Connected Account has insufficient funds.

12.4 **Dono never pays refunds from its own funds**, does not pursue external debt collection, and does not guarantee recovery.

12.5 If an approved refund is not paid: the responsible account holder remains contractually liable to you; Dono may suspend or permanently ban the user, remove their Campaigns, refer deliberate misuse to their institution, and report suspected fraud to the police or another authority; Dono may assemble evidence to support a chargeback in your favour; and Dono may share relevant identity information with you where lawful and reasonably necessary to enable you to pursue your remedies. You may also pursue chargeback, Payment Provider or legal remedies.

13.2 Dono's 21-day decision target may consume part of your card issuer's chargeback window. If timing matters to you, check your issuer's deadlines before using Dono's process first.

13.3 The Connected Account is intended to bear the chargeback and its Payment Provider consequences. Dono may collect and assist with dispute evidence. Who submits evidence and who pays dispute fees is **[SUBJECT TO STRIPE CONFIGURATION]**.

13.4 You must use refund and chargeback procedures honestly. Dishonest use is a breach of these Terms.

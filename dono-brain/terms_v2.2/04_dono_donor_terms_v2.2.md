### 2. Who can donate; currency

2.1 **Anyone may donate.** You do not need a Dono account, and there is no minimum age to make a Donation. You must be legally capable of authorising the payment and must be using a payment method you are entitled to use. You may be located anywhere, subject to Payment Provider availability, supported cards, sanctions, anti-fraud controls, local restrictions and any restrictions Dono adopts (clause 4.4 of the Terms of Service).

2.2 **Age at checkout.** Because donating is open to everyone, checkout asks you to confirm that **you are 18 or over**. This is a confirmation, not a verified age check, and we do not pretend otherwise. There is no parent-or-guardian-permission alternative to this confirmation. Note that **there is no minimum age to create a Dono account or to post a comment** — the 18-or-over declaration applies only to creating a Campaign, acting for a Society, and this checkout confirmation.

2.3 All Campaigns and Donations are in pounds sterling. If your card was issued outside the UK, your card issuer converts the amount at its own rate and may charge its own fees; Dono does not perform the conversion and does not receive any issuer charge. If you are refunded later, the refund is made in sterling for the amount you gave, and your issuer converts it back at the rate applying on the refund day, so the amount in your currency may differ slightly. This is outside Dono's and the Campaign Owner's control.

### 3. What your Donation is — and is not

3.1 A Donation is a **conditional contribution** to the stated campaign purpose. It is **not**: an investment; a loan; equity or an ownership interest; a payment for goods or services; a guarantee of any outcome; or automatically repayable because you are dissatisfied.

3.2 **How your contract is formed.** By publishing a Campaign, the named Campaign Owner makes a **standing offer** to accept eligible contributions on the terms shown on the Campaign page, and **appoints Dono as its disclosed agent** solely to present that offer and to communicate acceptance on the Campaign Owner's behalf. **Your contract with the named Campaign Owner forms when the Payment Provider confirms your payment and Dono sends your confirmation.** The Campaign Owner accepts your contribution subject to the published conditions, including using the funds only for the stated purpose and honouring the published refund triggers. **Dono is not a party to that contract** — Dono acts only as the Campaign Owner's agent for the purpose of forming it. Separately, you agree Dono's Terms for using the Platform, and the Campaign Owner agrees Dono's Terms for using the Platform.

3.3 **Who you are contracting with.** Before you confirm, checkout tells you: the Campaign; the Campaign Owner; for a Society Campaign, the verified representative acting for the Society; the account the funds will be paid into; and the contracting party. Your confirmation email repeats this and records the version of each document that applied. **[ENGINEERING — BUILD REQUIRED: the "You're donating to" panel.]**

3.4 **The Campaign Owner is the Beneficiary.** Dono does not permit fundraising for a third party. The funds are raised for the person or Society running the Campaign, and the funded property belongs to the person or body named in the Campaign's Ownership Statement.

3.5 Donations are not necessarily tax-deductible; Dono issues no charitable tax receipts and claims no Gift Aid. Taxes are your responsibility.

3.6 **Commercial and entrepreneurial campaigns** are permitted **only** where no reward, financial return, repayment, priority access, discount, product, service or other benefit is offered, and they are marked on the page. If you donate to one you receive no equity, ownership, repayment right or financial return; any value created belongs to the Campaign Owner. Consider this before donating.

### 4. Who receives your money

4.1 Your Donation is processed by the Payment Provider and paid to the **Recipient's** Connected Account — the person or organisation that connected the Stripe account for the Campaign. **Dono never receives or holds your donation; Dono receives only its platform fee.** For Society Campaigns, the campaign page discloses whether the Recipient is the Society's own account, a college or university account, a registered charity holding funds for the Society, or a named representative's account.

### 5. Making a Donation, cancellation information and recurring gifts

5.1 Donations are charged when you confirm and are collected even if the target is not met. You must provide correct payment information and be authorised to use the payment method. The Payment Provider's terms and privacy policy also apply.

5.2 **Cancellation of the Dono service element.** Where you choose to pay Dono's fee (by covering fees), you enter a paid distance service contract with Dono for that element, and Dono is your supplier for it. Before you confirm we tell you the price of that service, that it is optional, and how to contact us. Dono's service is performed immediately when your donation is processed. If you ask us to begin providing that service immediately and acknowledge that you will lose your right to cancel once it is fully performed, we act on that request; we ask for that request and acknowledgement in a **separate, unticked control**. Where our service is not fully performed, you retain the right to cancel within 14 days and to a proportionate refund of the fee for what has been performed. **This does not affect the separate conditional contribution to the Campaign or your rights against the Campaign Owner.** **[SOLICITOR SIGN-OFF — Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 wording, model cancellation form and the immediate-performance acknowledgement.]**

5.4 **Recurring Donations.** You may set up a Donation that repeats monthly until you cancel. If the Campaign closes or is removed, further charges stop. Each charge is a Donation and carries the same refund rights as a one-off gift.

### 7. Receipts

7.1 You receive an email **donation confirmation**, showing: the amount; the date; the Campaign; the Campaign Owner and the Recipient who received the money; Dono's platform fee, shown separately; any fee-cover amount you chose; any currency-conversion cost; and the version of each document that applied. ### 8. Hiding your name

8.1 You may choose to **hide your name from public display** on Dono. This hides your name from the public campaign page. **It does not hide information the Payment Provider makes available to the Recipient, or to card networks, banks or authorities.**

8.2 **What this does not do.** Your donation amount remains publicly displayed, and unique amounts or timing can sometimes identify a donor in a small community. Dono always holds your name against the payment. Because your payment is charged directly to the Recipient's own Stripe account, **the person or Society that operates that account may be able to see the transaction details the Payment Provider shows them, which can include your name — and Dono cannot guarantee otherwise.** We explain this when you select the option. What Dono controls is that **Dono does not disclose your identity to the Campaign Owner as a dispute-resolution step** (clause 11.5), and the Campaign Terms prohibit a Campaign Owner from using payment information to identify, contact or pressure you.

8.3 Comments always display a name; hidden-name commenting is not available, so if you comment you identify yourself as a supporter. You may change the setting at any time, affecting future display only; it cannot recall anything already seen, screenshotted, cached or archived. A Campaign Owner cannot thank you personally on the Platform if your name is hidden.

### 9. What you can see about spending

9.1 Campaign Owners submit receipts privately to Dono and publish updates and a Closure Statement. **Receipts are not published or shown to Donors** (they often contain personal information). You instead see a factual **evidence status**: "Documents received; line-item amounts matched to budget; documents not authenticated", *evidence outstanding*, or *evidence overdue*. The first means only that Dono received evidence for the declared expenditure and matched line-item amounts to the budget. It is **not** a statement that money was spent properly, goods were delivered, a price was reasonable, or a document is authentic. Form your own judgement.

### 10. Partial funding, changes and surplus

10.1 If a Campaign does not reach target, the Campaign Owner decides whether the Project can proceed provided the money can still be meaningfully used; otherwise refunds are arranged.

10.2 **Material Changes** follow the three-tier matrix in clause 14 of the Terms of Service. For a *material but equivalent* change you are notified individually and have **14 days to request a refund**. A *fundamental* change means the Campaign is cancelled and refunds are required unless you expressly opt in. The Campaign Owner must freeze affected spending until the refund window closes. **Dono cannot freeze funds technically** — that is a contractual obligation on the Campaign Owner, and if funds have already been spent your refund depends on what the Campaign Owner still holds. Clause 12 explains this honestly.

10.3 **Surplus.** A Campaign cannot be funded beyond its target, so over-funding does not arise and any over-payment is refunded in full. Surplus arising because the Campaign Owner cannot reasonably use the money for the stated purpose is refunded in **reverse chronological order** (most recent Donor first), not proportionately, so no Donor is guaranteed a share. This is disclosed at checkout. A small residual amount below a stated threshold need not be refunded; where that happens it stays dedicated to the Campaign's stated purpose, is never kept by Dono, and is declared in the Closure Statement. **[TO BE CALCULATED BY DONO TEAM — the threshold.]**

### 11. Refunds

11.1 **Grounds** include: duplicate payment; payment error; unauthorised payment; campaign cancellation; material misrepresentation, including an untrue statement about matched funding; fraud; misuse of funds; failure to provide required evidence; a material unauthorised change; inability to use the money meaningfully; failure to proceed where funds should be returned; or verification failure. Mere dissatisfaction is insufficient.

11.2 **Unauthorised payments — act fast.** If your payment was unauthorised, contact your card issuer or payment provider **at once**: your claim is against them and may require a refund by the end of the next business day, subject to statutory exceptions. Dono will preserve evidence and notify the Payment Provider, but Dono's internal process is optional and does not replace or delay your statutory rights.

11.3 **Time limits** (for Dono's voluntary internal process only — these do not shorten your legal rights): an ordinary request within 60 days after the Campaign End Date or after you became aware of the matter, whichever is later; a fraud or deliberate-misrepresentation request up to 12 months after the Donation. Dono may accept late cases where fair, and latent-fraud and legal rights are preserved.

11.4 **How Dono decides.** Dono determines, on the balance of probabilities, whether under the Platform's rules the Campaign Owner is required to issue a refund, considering all relevant evidence. **Some grounds are objective and do not require you to show that the issue affected your decision to donate** — a duplicate payment, a payment error, an unauthorised payment, a donation by a child without permission, and cancellation of the Campaign fall into this category. For grounds that depend on the Campaign Owner's conduct — misrepresentation, misuse of funds, an unauthorised Material Change, failure to provide evidence — a refund is granted where the issue **materially affected** the donation and there is a **causal connection** between the breach and the refund. Partial refunds may be given, and more than one ground may apply at once. Staff with a conflict of interest do not decide the case. Dono aims to acknowledge within two Working Days, decides within 21 calendar days of a complete request, and may extend once by up to a further 21 days with reasons. **External deadlines — including card-scheme chargeback windows and statutory limitation periods — continue independently of Dono's process.**

11.5 **Identity in a dispute.** A Campaign Owner is told the substance of your allegation and shown the evidence, but is not told your identity by Dono where you hid your name. Disclosure occurs only where required or permitted by law (clause 25 of the Terms of Service). Clause 8.2 explains what the Payment Provider may separately show them.

11.6 **Appeals** may be brought within ten Working Days and are decided under the single appeal framework in clause 8 of the Community Guidelines.

11.7 **Fees on a refund.** Where a refund is due and you were not at fault, **Dono refunds its own platform fee**; where you caused the refund through your own error, Dono's fee is not refunded. On a partial refund, Dono's fee is refunded proportionately where you were not at fault. Any fee-cover amount you paid is treated the same way as Dono's fee. Dono returns its fee through the Payment Provider's application-fee mechanism; the Payment Provider's own processing cost is not returned by Dono and is borne as set out in the Refund and Dispute Policy.

### 12. How an approved refund is paid

12.1 **Dono determines that a refund is required; the Recipient pays it** from their own Stripe account. **Dono does not initiate refunds of Donations, does not reach into a Campaign Owner's payment account, and holds no reserve power to do so.** Dono has sanctions and escalation routes against a Campaign Owner who refuses, but **cannot compel a refund to happen or guarantee recovery**, and never pays refunds from its own funds. A refund may be delayed or fail where the Connected Account has insufficient funds. **Please take this limitation into account before you donate.**

12.2 If a refund you are entitled to is not paid: the responsible account holder remains contractually liable to you; Dono may suspend or ban the user, remove their Campaigns, prevent them fundraising on Dono in future, refer deliberate misuse to their institution, and report suspected fraud; and Dono may assemble evidence to support a chargeback in your favour. **Dono cannot hold, delay or divert a payout from the Campaign Owner's account.** You may also pursue chargeback, Payment Provider or legal remedies.

### 13. Chargebacks

13.1 A chargeback is separate from Dono's process and is decided by your card issuer, not Dono. Dono's internal decision does not remove your statutory, payment-provider or legal rights.

13.2 If you are dissatisfied or believe a campaign has breached these Terms, **please contact Dono first** — in most cases we can investigate and, where appropriate, arrange a refund more quickly than a card dispute. A chargeback should generally be a last resort if the issue cannot be resolved directly with us. **This is guidance, not a restriction.**

13.3 If you choose to raise a chargeback with your card issuer, your issuer decides whether it is available under its rules. **Using a lawful chargeback does not breach these Terms.** Please give accurate information in any refund request or chargeback; knowingly making a false or fraudulent claim may result in suspension of your account and other appropriate action.

13.4 Dono's 21-day decision target may consume part of your card issuer's chargeback window; if timing matters, check your issuer's deadlines. The Recipient's Connected Account bears the chargeback, the Payment Provider's dispute fee, and the responsibility for submitting evidence.

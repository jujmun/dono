## Which process is this?

Dono has three complaint processes with three different statutory clocks. They share **one intake address**, and it is Dono's job — not the complainant's — to route the complaint correctly.

| If the complaint is about… | It is handled under… | Acknowledgement |
|---|---|---|
| How Dono has used, held, shared or deleted personal data; a subject-access or erasure request handled badly; a data breach | **This workflow** — the statutory data-protection complaints duty in force from 19 June 2026 | **Within 30 days** |
| Content, moderation, removal, suspension, a report not acted on, or Dono's online-safety duties | **Community Guidelines clause 8** | **Within 5 Working Days** |
| Anything else — fees, delays, technical faults, verification, how a process was handled | **Complaints Policy** | **Within 2 Working Days** |

Where a complaint spans more than one, run the process with the shortest acknowledgement clock for the acknowledgement, and answer each element under its own process. Tell the complainant that is what you are doing.

## Process

1. **Receive.** Complaints arrive at **`joindono.team@gmail.com`**, through the form below, or through any other channel a person uses to raise a data-protection concern. **A complaint does not have to use the word "complaint" or cite the legislation** — treat "why do you still have my details" as a complaint.

2. **Acknowledge — within 30 days of receipt.** Confirm receipt and give a brief indication of next steps. Use the standard text below.

3. **Investigate.** Review the complaint against the relevant processing activity in the ROPA — the ROPA is the reference for what Dono processes, why, on what basis, who receives it and how long it is kept. Determine what happened and identify any remedial action.

4. **Communicate the outcome.** Respond with the outcome and any action taken. Where you are refusing something, say why and how to escalate.

5. **Target resolution within 30 days where reasonably possible.** More complex matters may take longer; if so, keep the complainant informed of progress.

6. **Log.** Record every complaint — date received, nature, who handled it, outcome, dates of acknowledgement and resolution — for accountability and to spot recurring issues. Retained for six years from closure (ROPA row 15).

7. **Escalate where relevant.** If the complaint concerns a possible data breach, run the Incident Response Plan **in parallel** — do not wait for the complaint process to conclude. Tell the complainant they may also complain to the ICO.

8. **Who handles it.** **Amrit** handles data-protection complaints; **Sashank** covers in Amrit's absence and must be briefed on this workflow before launch; **Joe** is the second backup. Where the complaint concerns a decision Amrit made, **Sashank handles it in the first instance.**

## Standard response text

> We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days of receiving it. We will investigate it appropriately and respond without undue delay. We aim to resolve most complaints within 30 days where reasonably possible, but more complex matters may take longer. If that happens, we will keep you informed of our progress. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113 — you do not have to come to us first, though we would rather you did.

### 14. Use of funds; changes; surplus

14.1 **Use of funds.** Campaign funds may be used only for the purposes represented to Donors, including approved line items and reasonable directly related expenditure. Funds may be moved between verified line items within the same stated purpose.

14.2 **Partial funding.** If a Campaign does not meet its target, the Campaign Owner decides whether the Project can proceed with the amount raised, provided it can still be meaningfully used for the stated purpose; otherwise refunds must be arranged.

14.3 **Material Change — definition.** A **Material Change** is any change that a reasonable donor would consider important when deciding whether to make the donation.

14.4 **Material Change — three categories and remedy.**

(a) **Minor changes** — no donor consent required (for example: supplier changes, a different manufacturer, colour changes, small budget reallocations, timetable changes).
(b) **Material but equivalent changes** — Dono is notified, each affected donor is notified individually, and donors have **14 days to request a refund** (for example: different equipment, a different contractor, significant budget reallocations, a different venue).
(c) **Fundamental changes** — the Campaign is cancelled and refunds are required unless donors expressly opt in (for example: changing the purpose entirely, changing the Campaign Owner, changing the organisation, or using funds for operating expenses instead of the advertised project). Changing the Beneficiary is not possible, because the Beneficiary is the Campaign Owner (clause 3.1).

**Freezing spend.** Affected expenditure must be frozen until the refund window closes, so that donors entitled to refunds are not defeated by immediate spending. **Dono cannot freeze funds technically — this is a contractual obligation on the Campaign Owner**, and breaching it is a ground for enforcement under Part 10 and for a refund. Where funds have already been paid out and spent, a refund depends on the Campaign Owner's remaining funds; clause 13.1 and the Refund and Dispute Policy state the practical limits honestly. **A Material Change must be notified and approved before it is implemented, and may not be made after funds have been spent in order to regularise that spending.**

14.5 **Surplus Funds.** Any amount that cannot reasonably be used for the stated purpose must be refunded. Because proportionate micro-refunds are often uneconomic, **surplus is refunded in reverse chronological order** — the most recent Donor first, up to the amount they donated, then the next, until the surplus is exhausted. Surplus is not distributed proportionately, and no Donor is guaranteed a share. This is disclosed at checkout and in the Closure Statement, and Dono maintains a ledger of every surplus refund.

14.6 **De minimis.** A Campaign cannot be funded beyond its target, so **over-funding does not arise and no de minimis applies to it — every penny of any over-payment is refunded.** Surplus arising from **under-spend** is different: a residual amount below a stated threshold need not be refunded, because the Payment Provider's fixed per-transaction charge can make a very small refund cost more than the refund itself. A residual amount retained under this clause **remains dedicated to the Campaign's stated purpose and must be applied to it by the Campaign Owner**; it is never retained by Dono as revenue, and it must be declared in the Closure Statement. **[TO BE CALCULATED BY DONO TEAM — set the under-spend threshold by reference to the objective per-transaction cost, and disclose it at checkout and closure.]**

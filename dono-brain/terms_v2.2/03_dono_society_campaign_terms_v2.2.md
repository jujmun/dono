# Dono Society Campaign Terms

**Version:** Working draft v2.2 — 31 July 2026
**Status:** DRAFT — NOT FOR PUBLICATION. Applies the counsel review, the founder's amendments and the engineering configuration answers of 31 July 2026. Requires final UK solicitor sign-off, including the allocation of obligations within unincorporated associations. Open items marked **[SOLICITOR SIGN-OFF]** or **[ENGINEERING — BUILD REQUIRED]**.

> **What changed from v2.1:** the contracting model is settled as the **officer contracting model** — the Society is responsible, and where the Society cannot bear legal responsibility because of its legal status, the Society Representative accepts those obligations **personally and automatically**; there is **one Society Representative**, not two Responsible Individuals, and the second person is a **Secondary Contact** who may take over rather than a second approver; the **Society holds the Connected Account** and it is not transferred on succession, so the mandatory balance-transfer step is removed; the claim that Dono can **withhold payouts is removed**; the **Society is a business user** for liability purposes; the **Beneficiary is the Society**; the constitutional-approval bands are aligned with the single-approver rule.

---

### 1. About these Terms and who contracts

1.1 These Society Campaign Terms apply to Campaigns run on behalf of a Society, to the Society, to the Society Representative, to the Secondary Contact, and to any student whose Connected Account receives Donations for a Society Campaign. They supplement the Terms of Service and the Student Campaign Terms and take precedence for Society Campaigns where they conflict.

1.2 **The contracting model.**

(a) Every Society Campaign has **one Society Representative** — the individual (for example the President or Treasurer) who enters into these Terms **for and on behalf of the Society and its members**. Dono records their full name, university email and role.
(b) The Society Representative **warrants** that they are authorised under the Society's constitution or governing arrangements to create the Campaign, to authorise the receipt of Donations, and to accept these Terms on the Society's behalf.
(c) **The Society is responsible for complying with these Terms.** Where the Society is not capable of bearing legal responsibility, or those obligations cannot be enforced against it because of its legal status — for example because it is an unincorporated association without separate legal personality — **the Society Representative accepts those obligations personally**, to the extent permitted by law. This applies automatically and does not require a court to infer it.
(d) If the Society is incorporated (for example a charitable incorporated organisation or a company), the incorporated entity is the contracting party and clause 1.2(c) does not apply.

1.3 **A Society is typically an unincorporated association and is not necessarily a separate legal person.** Clause 1.2(c) is what makes the obligations in these Terms enforceable in that case. **[SOLICITOR SIGN-OFF — the enforceability of clause 1.2(c) against a Society Representative, and the allocation of property within unincorporated associations.]**

1.4 **The Society is the Beneficiary.** Under clause 3.1 of the Terms of Service the Beneficiary of a Campaign is its Campaign Owner. For a Society Campaign, the Campaign Owner and Beneficiary are the **Society**, operating through its Society Representative. Funds are raised for the Society, and the funded property or output belongs to the person or body recorded in the Ownership Statement under clause 3.3.

1.5 **The Society is a business user.** For the purposes of clauses 27 and 28 of the Terms of Service, a Society acting through its officers in the conduct of its organised activities is a business user, not a consumer. Clause 27.3 (exclusion of indirect loss and the capped liability) and clause 28.2 (indemnity) therefore apply. **[SOLICITOR SIGN-OFF — whether an unincorporated student society is properly treated as a business user under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.]**

1.6 Society approval, and the appearance of a Society or institution name on a Campaign, does not mean the university, college or institution operates, sponsors or endorses the Campaign or Dono.

### 2. Creating and approving a Society Campaign

2.1 A student may fundraise on behalf of a Society but not on behalf of another individual. A Society Campaign must be approved through the relevant society page before it is presented as a Society Campaign; nothing becomes public automatically, and every Campaign completes the mandatory pre-publication validation and human review.

2.2 **Authority declaration.** On approval the Society Representative actively confirms: *"I confirm that I am authorised under the Society's constitution or governing arrangements to create this Campaign, to authorise the receipt of Donations, and to accept these Terms on behalf of the Society and its members."* Dono does not require the constitution to be supplied every time.

2.3 **One approver is sufficient; the evidence of constitutional approval scales with the amount.** The Society registers one named Society Representative, whose approval alone is enough for Dono to publish the Campaign. Dono does not require a second person to approve. Separately, and as **evidence** that the Society itself has authorised the Campaign under its own rules, the Society Representative must supply:

| Funding target | Evidence of the Society's own approval |
|---|---|
| Under £2,500 | The Society Representative's confirmation that the committee has approved the Campaign |
| £2,500 – £10,000 | Written confirmation from two current committee officers |
| Over £10,000 | Formal committee approval — minutes, a written resolution, or equivalent evidence permitted by the Society's constitution |

This is evidence of the Society's internal authorisation, not a second Dono approval. Dono may request further evidence at any tier where a Campaign appears unusually risky. **Satisfying these checks does not mean Dono endorses the Campaign or guarantees that the Society has complied with its own constitution.** **[CONFIRM — these bands are a commercial risk decision; sign off the figures before publication.]**

2.4 The label "Society approved" means only that the society page approved the Campaign for presentation as a society campaign. It is not an institutional endorsement and not a Dono verification of any campaign statement.

2.5 Society Campaigns must comply with the permitted and prohibited categories in clause 8 of the Terms of Service. Religious-society activities are permitted; alcohol-related expenditure is prohibited, including as part of a society event.

### 3. Payment Recipient and asset ownership

3.1 **Who receives the funds.** Campaign setup asks who will receive the funds. The Recipient is whoever connects the Stripe account. **Ordinarily this is the Society's own Connected Account**, which is held by the Society and operated by its authorised officers, and which does not change when the Society Representative changes. It may instead be a College or University account, or a registered charity holding funds for the Society (clause 8.5 of the Terms of Service). Donations are paid to the Recipient's Connected Account.

3.2 The Recipient confirms they are authorised to receive the funds, authorised to run the Campaign, and responsible for refunds and chargebacks associated with their Connected Account. One legal regime applies to all Recipients.

3.3 **Ownership Statement.** At setup the Campaign records who will legally own the funded property or output — for example the Society, the College, the University, a charity, a trustee, or named office-holders. **The Society Representative warrants that the Ownership Statement is accurate and that they have authority to make it.** Items or outputs funded by a Society Campaign belong to the recorded owner and must be used for the stated purpose. **An outgoing officer must not retain funded property personally**, and the proceeds of any sale remain dedicated to society purposes.

3.4 **The Recipient, the Campaign Owner and the owner of the funded property may be three different persons.** Campaign setup records each separately: the Campaign Owner (the Society); the Recipient (the holder of the Connected Account); and the legal owner of the funded property under the Ownership Statement. Where any of them is not the Society, Dono may require an acknowledgement from that person.

3.5 Whether a given Society can in practice open the required Stripe connected account is confirmed before its first Campaign is published. **[ENGINEERING / FOUNDER — no evidence yet exists that an unincorporated society has successfully completed Connect onboarding. ### 4. Responsibilities and refunds

4.1 The Society Representative must: maintain and privately submit evidence within 14 days of expenditure (redacted per clause 7.2 of the Student Campaign Terms); publish required updates and a Closure Statement; notify Material Changes **before** implementation and freeze affected expenditure until the refund window closes; respond within **ten Working Days**, with a reasonable extension available on request; and cooperate with refunds and investigations.

4.2 **Execution of refunds.** The Connected Account holder is responsible for refunds and chargebacks and must execute any refund the Donor is entitled to promptly from their own Stripe dashboard. **Dono does not initiate refunds of Donations and holds no reserve power to do so.** Where a refund is due and the Donor was not at fault, Dono separately refunds its own platform fee — that is Dono returning its own money.

4.3 The Connected Account is charged the Payment Provider's processing fees and its dispute fee (currently £20 per dispute in the UK), **owns any dispute, and is responsible for submitting evidence** to the Payment Provider. Dono will assist by providing records it holds, on request.

4.4 Receipts and evidence are held privately and not shown to Donors; the campaign page shows only the factual evidence status.

4.5 **Donor identity** is not disclosed by Dono to the Recipient or the Society as a dispute-resolution step; disclosure occurs only where required or permitted by law (clause 25 of the Terms of Service). **Separately**, because Donations are charged directly to the Society's Connected Account, the Payment Provider may show the account's authorised officers transaction details including a Donor's name, whatever their display preference on Dono. That is outside Dono's control, and using it to identify, contact or pressure a Donor who chose to hide their name is a breach of these Terms. The Society must limit access to its Connected Account to officers who need it.

### 5. Resale and disposal

5.1 The disposal rules in clause 8 of the Student Campaign Terms apply: no acquisition intended for immediate resale or personal profit; **no prior approval is required to dispose**, but any disposal of a material asset must be notified to Dono within 14 days with the reason, proceeds and application; pre-completion sale proceeds must be reapplied or refunded; disposal after genuine use is permitted. Proceeds remain dedicated to society purposes and may not be retained by an individual officer.

### 6. Succession and change of representative

6.1 **One Society Representative and one Secondary Contact.** Every Society Campaign registers **one Society Representative**, who is the legal contact, is responsible for campaign compliance, updates, receipts and responding to Dono, and is the person who accepted these Terms. Every Society Campaign also registers **one Secondary Contact** — a named current officer who can take over as Society Representative if the primary leaves office. **The Secondary Contact is not a second approver**, is not required to approve anything, and does not need to complete verification unless and until they become the Society Representative.

6.2 **Managed change of representative.** Dono may approve a change of Society Representative where the Society demonstrates appropriate authority and the incoming representative completes Dono's verification. **Any change affects only future responsibility and does not alter responsibility for transactions processed before the change.**

6.3 **The Connected Account normally does not change.** Where the Society (or a College, University or charity) holds the Connected Account, succession is simply a change of authorised representative: **the Recipient does not change, no funds move, and no balance is transferred.** This is the ordinary case and the one Dono is designed around.

6.4 **Where a named individual holds the Connected Account.** If, exceptionally, the Connected Account is held personally by a named student rather than by the Society, succession is more limited and Dono will say so plainly at setup:

(a) the incoming representative is identity-verified and completes the authority declaration;
(b) the committee confirms the appointment under clause 6.5;
(c) the incoming representative connects their own Stripe account;
(d) **a balance cannot be migrated between connected accounts.** The Payment Provider does not support moving a balance from one connected account to another. Funds already received remain in the outgoing representative's Connected Account and must be dealt with by them under these Terms — spent on the campaign purpose, or refunded. Any transfer of money between the two people is a matter between them and the Society, outside Dono and outside the Payment Provider, and Dono does not require, arrange, supervise or guarantee it;
(e) future Donations are directed to the new Connected Account once the change is approved; and
(f) the outgoing representative **remains responsible for every transaction processed through their Connected Account before the change**, including refunds and chargebacks, and remains bound by clause 1.2(c).

Because of (d), Dono strongly prefers a Society-held Connected Account and may refuse to publish a Society Campaign on a personal Connected Account.

6.5 **Evidence of a change.** The Society confirms the change was made in line with its constitution, using this hierarchy: approved committee minutes or a written resolution (preferred); or written confirmation from two current officers, one of whom should ordinarily be the president, chair, treasurer or secretary; or, only where Dono can verify both individuals' roles, confirmation from the outgoing principal officer and one current or incoming officer; or another form of evidence Dono accepts following manual review. Each signatory confirms their name and committee role, that the Society has approved the change, who is authorised to operate the connected Stripe account, that the change does not conflict with the Society's constitution or any known institutional rules, and that the Society will tell Dono if approval is withdrawn or the authorised officers change again.

6.6 A change of Society Representative, of the Society, or of the Connected Account recipient is a Material Change and follows the Material Change process. **The Campaign is paused to new Donations while a change of Connected Account is being completed.** Departure does not extinguish a departing individual's accrued obligations, including any personal responsibility as Connected Account holder for pre-change transactions.

### 7. Failed recovery and enforcement

7.1 If a refund the Donor is entitled to is not executed, the Connected Account holder remains contractually liable, and the Society (or, under clause 1.2(c), the Society Representative personally) remains responsible for compliance with these Terms. Dono never pays refunds from its own funds and does not guarantee recovery. Dono may suspend or ban users, remove Campaigns, prevent future fundraising on Dono, refer deliberate misuse to the Institution under clause 23.7 of the Terms of Service, and report suspected fraud to the police or another authority. **Dono cannot withhold or delay a payout from a Connected Account and does not claim to be able to.**

7.2 Dono may warn, restrict, suspend, remove or ban the Campaign, the Society's presence, the Society Representative, the Secondary Contact or the Connected Account holder under Part 10 of the Terms of Service.

### 8. General

8.1 The liability, indemnity, changes, termination, governing-law, complaints and general provisions of the Terms of Service apply. **The Society is a business user (clause 1.5), so clause 27.3 and the indemnity in clause 28.2 apply**, and under clause 28.3 the indemnity is given by the Society and, where clause 1.2(c) operates, by the Society Representative personally.
